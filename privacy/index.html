Privacy Policy

Town of Salem is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our site, you agree to the practices described here.

We collect minimal data to operate our community features. When you post a comment, we store the displayed name you provide, the comment text, its moderation status, and a one-way visitor hash. This hash is generated from your browser or device and does not reveal your identity to us. We do not store raw IP addresses for comments.

Our ratings feature uses an anonymous first-party cookie named af_visitor and a one-way hash to recognize repeat visitors and prevent abuse. This cookie does not contain personal information and is not used for advertising. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising services, and we do not sell or share your personal data with outside parties.

We may use aggregated, non-identifying information to improve our services and understand general usage patterns. This data cannot be traced back to you.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. Your continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at [contact_email]. We will respond to reasonable requests to access, correct, or delete your personal information.

This policy is an informational draft and does not constitute legal advice. You should consult a qualified professional for compliance with applicable laws.
