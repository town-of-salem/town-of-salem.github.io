🔒 Privacy

Privacy policy

Town of Salem is designed as a fan guide site. External game content is loaded only when you choose to launch it.

Static site behavior

The site itself does not require an account, does not process Town of Salem credentials, and does not proxy game login or payment information. Your hosting provider may still keep ordinary server access logs.

External game frames

Game pages use click-to-load iframes. Before you click Play, the external game is not requested by the frame. After you click, the external provider may receive normal browser information such as IP address, user agent, and cookies according to that provider’s own policies.

Analytics and audience measurement

This fan site uses Google Analytics and LiveInternet for audience measurement. These services can receive page URLs, referrers, browser and device details, and network information under their own privacy and cookie practices.

Advertising

Google AdSense code loads when a page opens. Advertising services may receive browser, device and network information and may use cookies. Advertising requests are separate from the game window: you do not need to press Play for the advertising script to load.
