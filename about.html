About Town of Salem

The original Town of Salem combines hidden roles, night abilities and public trials. Its Coven expansion adds another faction and new ways to investigate, protect and deceive.

A reference for the original Town of Salem

This fan site covers the original Town of Salem and its Coven expansion. Role names and interactions from Town of Salem 2 are not interchangeable with the original game. The pages here focus on night actions, role claims, investigative feedback, voting and the decisions that connect them.

Find the answer that fits your question

Use the role library for an ability, restriction or cover claim. Open mechanics for shared rules such as trials and defense. The guides turn those rules into decisions: whom to investigate, when to reveal information and how to plan the next vote.

A new player can begin with How to Play, then practice a concise last will. A returning player may find a result table or the Coven reference more useful than another introduction. Mode pages explain which assumptions depend on the configured role list.

Rules, advice and editions

Ability descriptions concern what a role can do. Strategy sections explain possible uses of that ability, with examples rather than promises of a winning move. Patches can change role names, investigative groups and exceptional interactions; compare version notes and your current lobby before applying an older rule.
