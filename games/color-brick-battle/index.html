About Color Brick Battle

Color Brick Battle puts a colorful spin on the classic brick breaker. You control a paddle to bounce a ball and destroy bricks, but only those matching the ball's current color. The ball starts white and changes color when hitting the colored side of the paddle, so timing and positioning are everything.

Master the Color Change

In Color Brick Battle, the core loop is simple: move the paddle to keep the ball in play and aim it at bricks. The twist is that the ball's color changes when it touches the colored side of the paddle. You must align the ball's color with the bricks you want to destroy, making every hit a deliberate choice.

Precision and positioning are essential as you juggle the ball's color and trajectory. With a mix of reflexes and strategy, you'll need to plan your paddle movements to set up the right color for the next shot. It's a fast, addictive challenge that tests your ability to think and react quickly.

PLAY BETTER

Color Brick Battle tips

  1. Watch the ball's color after it hits the paddle to know which bricks you can target next.
  2. Move the paddle to the side just before the ball arrives to change its color intentionally.
  3. Plan your shots to clear clusters of same-colored bricks in one pass.
  4. Stay under the ball's trajectory to avoid missing, especially when the ball speeds up.

Color Brick Battle FAQ

How does the color mechanic work in Color Brick Battle?

The ball starts white and changes color when it touches the colored side of the paddle. Only bricks matching the ball's current color can be destroyed.

What are the controls for Color Brick Battle?

You can move the paddle by touching the screen and dragging, or by using the A and D keys on a keyboard.

What is the objective of Color Brick Battle?

The goal is to clear all the bricks by bouncing the ball and hitting them with the correct color. Precision and positioning are key to success.
