About Coffe Merge House

Coffee Merge House is a cozy merge game where you combine ingredients to prepare increasingly valuable treats and serve customers their requested orders. Your central objective is to earn coins, upgrade your café, unlock new generators, and expand your collection of delicious creations while managing limited board space.

Master the Merge Board

The core loop in Coffee Merge House revolves around generating items from your generators and merging them to create higher-tier products. Each merge frees up space and brings you closer to fulfilling customer orders. As you progress, you'll unlock new generators that produce different ingredients, allowing you to craft more complex and valuable treats.

Strategic planning is key because the board space is limited. You must decide which items to merge, which to keep for upcoming orders, and when to use your generators. Careful management ensures you can serve customers quickly and earn coins efficiently. Upgrading your café and expanding your collection keeps the gameplay fresh and rewarding.

PLAY BETTER

Coffe Merge House tips

  1. Always merge items in groups of five when possible to get the most value.
  2. Keep a few low-level items on hand in case a customer requests them.
  3. Prioritize unlocking new generators to expand your product variety.
  4. Plan your board layout to avoid blocking generators with high-level items.
QUICK ANSWERS

Coffe Merge House FAQ

What is the main objective in Coffee Merge House?

The main objective is to prepare delicious products, complete customer orders, earn coins, and grow your café by merging items and unlocking new generators.

How do I get new items in Coffee Merge House?

You get new items by clicking on generators. Each generator produces a specific type of item that you can then merge with others to create higher-tier products.

What happens when I merge items in Coffee Merge House?

Merging two identical items creates a higher-level product, which is often more valuable and can be used to fulfill more complex customer orders.
