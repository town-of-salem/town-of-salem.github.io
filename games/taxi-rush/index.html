About Taxi Rush

Taxi Rush is a driving simulation game where you speed through busy city streets to pick up and deliver clients as quickly and safely as possible. Your main goal is to satisfy each passenger while navigating dense traffic, avoiding roadblocks, and earning rewards that let you improve your taxi.

Mastering Traffic and Client Demands

Each run in Taxi Rush drops you into a dynamic urban environment filled with moving cars and sudden obstacles. You'll need to weave through lanes, brake at the right moments, and keep an eye on your passenger's patience as you race toward the drop-off point. Successful deliveries reward you with in-game currency and progress.

As you earn rewards, you can upgrade and customize your taxi to boost its performance and style. Different clients may have unique demands, so you'll need to adapt your driving approach on the fly. Strategizing your route through the city helps you avoid congestion and roadblocks, while consistent performance pushes you higher on the leaderboard.

PLAY BETTER

Taxi Rush tips

  1. Anticipate traffic patterns by scanning ahead and easing off the accelerator early.
  2. Use the left mouse button alongside WAD for finer steering adjustments in tight spots.
  3. Prioritize safe driving over pure speed; crashes and delays reduce your reward.
  4. Watch for roadblocks and plan an alternate lane before you're forced to stop.
  5. Upgrade your taxi's handling or acceleration first to make future deliveries smoother.
QUICK ANSWERS

Taxi Rush FAQ

What is the main objective in Taxi Rush?

Your goal is to pick up clients and deliver them to their destinations quickly and safely while navigating city traffic and avoiding roadblocks. Successful deliveries earn rewards that let you upgrade your taxi.

How do I control the taxi in Taxi Rush?

Use the W, A, and D keys to accelerate, steer left, and steer right. You can also use the left mouse button as an additional control input to help manage the car.

What happens if I hit traffic or roadblocks?

Collisions and roadblocks slow you down and can reduce your delivery efficiency. Avoiding them helps you complete trips faster, earn better rewards, and keep your passengers satisfied.
