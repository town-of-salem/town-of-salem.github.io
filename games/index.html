Choose the kind of deduction you enjoy

For social deduction games like Town of Salem with human opponents, start with Untrusted. Its NETSEC operation and undercover Agents make it the multiplayer choice here. You need an account, enough players and time for a shared session.

For focused solo logic, The Wrong Ones, Believe Me! and The False Few give you statements or abilities to test. Read the rules of each puzzle carefully: the number of liars, the meaning of a result and the cost of an accusation differ. Unlike multiplayer hidden role games, these puzzles use fixed clues instead of human bluffing.

For a longer investigation, LAN Party ’97 asks you to connect identities with written and visual clues. Dungeon Descent lets you reconstruct a fantasy story by revealing past scenes. Both reward patient evidence reading, without a live lobby or a social vote.

If you want the day-and-night format of online Mafia games, open Town of Salem. The five solo games above do not reproduce a multiplayer Mafia or Werewolf match. For your next Salem game, explore scumreading and clear communication.
