About Avatar Life My Town

Avatar Life My Town is a casual simulator game for kids that turns a dollhouse into a stage for sweet home stories. Meet fun family characters, role-play house life, and manage your new home. Your main goal is to take good care of your character and prepare delicious food, including cooking a big meal.

Cooking and Caring

The core loop of Avatar Life My Town centers on daily family routines. You'll interact with the characters, keep them happy, and head to the kitchen to prepare meals. Cooking a big meal is a key activity, and you'll combine ingredients and follow simple steps to serve up tasty dishes that keep everyone satisfied.

As you play, you'll balance caring for your character with managing the household. The game encourages creativity and role-play, letting you decide how the story unfolds. Whether you're cooking a big meal or just hanging out, the fun comes from experimenting with different actions and seeing how the family reacts.

PLAY BETTER

Avatar Life My Town tips

  1. Interact with every character to discover their needs and preferences.
  2. Prioritize cooking a big meal early to keep everyone happy.
  3. Experiment with different ingredients to see what dishes you can make.
  4. Take your time to explore the dollhouse and find hidden interactions.
