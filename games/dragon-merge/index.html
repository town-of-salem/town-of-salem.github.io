About Dragon Merge

Dragon Merge is a casual puzzle game where you combine identical dragons to discover new and stronger creatures. Your goal is to collect every dragon and ultimately obtain the last one. Along the way, you'll use your growing collection to defeat monsters in the battle arena.

Mastering the Merge Mechanic

The core loop in Dragon Merge revolves around merging. You start with basic dragons and combine two identical ones to create a new, more powerful dragon. Each merge expands your collection, unlocking visually distinct dragons that are essential for progressing through the game.

Beyond merging, you'll take your dragons into the battle arena to fight monsters. Your collection's strength directly influences your success, so strategic merging is key. The game is designed to be easy to pick up but offers a neverending challenge as you strive to obtain the final dragon.

PLAY BETTER

Dragon Merge tips

  1. Always merge dragons of the same type to unlock new, stronger dragons.
  2. Prioritize merging to build a balanced collection before entering battles.
  3. In the arena, aim carefully with your mouse to hit monsters effectively.
  4. Keep an eye on your dragon collection to know which merges are available.
QUICK ANSWERS

Dragon Merge FAQ

How do I get new dragons in Dragon Merge?

You get new dragons by merging two identical dragons. When you merge, you discover a new, stronger dragon that is added to your collection.

What is the main objective in Dragon Merge?

The main objective is to merge dragons to build a powerful collection, defeat monsters in the battle arena, and ultimately obtain the last dragon in the game.

What controls do I need to use in Dragon Merge?

You use the left mouse button to shoot the dragon and to interact with in-game buttons. All actions are performed with the mouse.
