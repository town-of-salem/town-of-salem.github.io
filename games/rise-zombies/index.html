About Rise Zombies

Rise Zombies is an action-packed survival game set in a post-apocalyptic city overrun by the undead. Your goal is to fight off endless waves of zombies, collect gold from fallen enemies, and upgrade your combat skills, speed, and defense to stay alive.

Upgrade to Outlast the Horde

In Rise Zombies, you move through the ruined city, engaging zombies in melee combat. Each defeated enemy drops gold, which you use to enhance your abilities. Prioritize upgrades that match your playstyle—whether boosting punch power, increasing speed to dodge, or fortifying defense to withstand more hits.

As waves progress, zombies become stronger and more numerous, forcing you to adapt your tactics. Quick reflexes and smart decisions about when to engage or retreat are key. Explore the environment, manage your resources, and steadily grow stronger to push deeper into the city and survive the relentless onslaught.

PLAY BETTER

Rise Zombies tips

  1. Keep moving to avoid being surrounded by zombies.
  2. Use kicks to knock back groups and create space.
  3. Prioritize upgrading speed early to dodge faster attacks.
  4. Collect gold quickly before new waves spawn.
  5. Balance upgrades between offense and defense based on your playstyle.
QUICK ANSWERS

Rise Zombies FAQ

What is the main objective in Rise Zombies?

Survive endless waves of zombies in a post-apocalyptic city by defeating them, collecting gold, and upgrading your combat skills, speed, and defense.

How do I control my character in Rise Zombies?

Use WASD or arrow keys to move, Z or J to punch, and X or K to kick.

How do I get stronger in Rise Zombies?

Defeat zombies to earn gold, then spend it on upgrades that increase your combat skills, speed, and defense.
