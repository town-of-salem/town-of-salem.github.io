About Not Your Seafood Salad

Not Your Seafood Salad flips the script on survival: you're the prey, and you're done with it. Armed with treasures washed ashore, you fight back against ravenous waves. Your goal is simple—survive. As you evolve and equip strange items, you grow stronger, turning the tide in this top-down arena.

Core Survival Loop

In Not Your Seafood Salad, you start each run by picking a hero from 15 characters, each with a distinct survival style. You'll then customize your build by choosing buffs that complement your playstyle and sacrificing those that would only weigh you down. The core loop is simple: move, dodge, and attack with whatever you've scavenged, all while managing your character's evolution.

The challenge ramps up across two modes. Warm Up offers a shorter session, while Adventure throws 20 waves of hunters at you. With over 100 items, weapons, and evolutions, every run offers new combinations. You'll need to adapt your strategy on the fly, deciding which buffs to keep and which to drop, as the waves grow more relentless.

PLAY BETTER

Not Your Seafood Salad tips

  1. Prioritize movement: staying mobile helps you dodge hunter attacks and position for counterattacks.
  2. Experiment with different buffs to find a combination that suits your playstyle; don't be afraid to sacrifice weak ones.
  3. In Adventure mode, pace yourself—20 waves require endurance, so manage your health and upgrades wisely.
  4. Use the mouse joystick on PC for precise aiming; on mobile, use swipe gestures to keep control.
