About Wiggly Worm Race

Wiggly Worm Race is a simple and super fun racing game for kids. Pick your worm, tap your button as fast as you can, and be the first to cross the finish line. Choose between 3 levels and play solo or race against up to 3 friends on the same screen.

Tap to Win the Race

In Wiggly Worm Race, the core loop is all about speed and timing. Each player picks a worm, then the race begins. The faster you tap your assigned button, the quicker your worm wiggles toward the finish line. It's a test of finger speed and endurance, with the winner being the first to cross.

The game offers three levels, so you can start easy and work your way up. You can play solo to practice your tapping or challenge up to three friends on the same screen. The friendly competition and simple mechanics make it a great choice for kids and families looking for a quick, fun race.

PLAY BETTER

Wiggly Worm Race tips

  1. Tap with alternating fingers to increase your speed.
  2. Stay relaxed to keep a steady rhythm.
  3. If playing with friends, agree on the same level for a fair race.
  4. Practice solo to improve your tapping speed before a multiplayer match.

Wiggly Worm Race FAQ

How do you play Wiggly Worm Race?

Pick a worm, choose a level, and tap your assigned button as fast as you can to move your worm toward the finish line. The first to cross wins.

What are the controls for Wiggly Worm Race?

Player 1 uses the Arrow Keys, Player 2 uses A and S, Player 3 uses V and B, and Player 4 uses L and K. You can also tap the right and left sides of the screen.

Can you play Wiggly Worm Race with friends?

Yes, you can race against up to 3 friends on the same screen. Each player uses their own set of keys.
