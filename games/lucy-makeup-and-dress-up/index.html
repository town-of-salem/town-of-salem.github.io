About Lucy: Makeup and Dress up

In Lucy: Makeup and Dress up, kids join Lucy from Wolfoo for a colorful makeover adventure. The game offers two modes: Makeup, where you can style Lucy's hair, nails, and makeup, and Dress Up, where you create princess party looks. It's a safe, creative space for young fashion lovers.

Two Modes of Creative Play

Start in Makeup mode to give Lucy a fresh look. Choose eyeshadow, lipstick, hairstyles, and sparkly nail designs. Then switch to Dress Up mode to mix and match dresses, skirts, and accessories. With hundreds of cute items, you can build a unique outfit for any party.

The game encourages experimentation and personal style. As you play, you'll unlock new outfits and items, giving you more options to design your favorite look. The bright, kid-friendly visuals and simple controls make it easy for young players to jump in and enjoy makeover fun without any scary content.

PLAY BETTER

Lucy: Makeup and Dress up tips

  1. Try combining bright colors in makeup for a fun look.
  2. In Dress Up mode, mix different styles to see what looks best.
  3. Unlock new outfits by playing more and experimenting.
  4. Use the sparkly nail options to add extra shine to your design.
QUICK ANSWERS

Lucy: Makeup and Dress up FAQ

What is Lucy: Makeup and Dress up about?

It's a beauty and fashion game for kids where you can give Lucy a makeover in Makeup mode and dress up princesses in Dress Up mode.

How do I control the game?

You can play using a mouse click or by tapping on the screen, making it easy for young children.

Are there different modes in the game?

Yes, there are two modes: Makeup mode for applying makeup, hairstyles, and nails, and Dress Up mode for styling outfits and accessories.
