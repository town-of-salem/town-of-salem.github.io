About Mine Keeper

In Mine Keeper, you step into the boots of a dwarf lord with a mission to forge a thriving kingdom. This simulation game blends town building, resource management, and adventure as you gather fruits, sell them for food, and construct facilities. Your ultimate goal is to grow your nation and secure its prosperity.

Balancing Growth and Defense

Your daily routine in Mine Keeper revolves around collecting fruits and selling them to feed your people. With the income, you can upgrade houses, build new facilities, and expand your town. As your kingdom gains fame, more dwarves will arrive, ready to work as farmers or soldiers, so you must manage your workforce wisely to keep everything running smoothly.

Venturing into the mines is where the real challenge begins. Deep below, valuable stones and gems await, but so do monsters. Always bring your troops along for protection. Deciding when to invest in military strength versus civic improvements is key, as a well-defended and prosperous kingdom will attract even more inhabitants and opportunities.

PLAY BETTER

Mine Keeper tips

  1. Always keep a steady supply of food by selling fruits regularly, as your people depend on it.
  2. Invest in better houses early to attract more dwarves, which increases your workforce.
  3. Before mining, ensure you have soldiers in your party to fend off monsters.
  4. Balance your spending between new facilities and military upgrades to maintain both growth and safety.
QUICK ANSWERS

Mine Keeper FAQ

What is the main objective in Mine Keeper?

Your goal is to build and manage a prosperous dwarf kingdom by collecting and selling fruits, constructing facilities, and mining for valuable stones and gems while defending against monsters.

How do I get more dwarves to join my kingdom?

As your kingdom becomes known and you provide better houses and facilities, more people will move in to live and work, allowing you to hire farmers and soldiers.

What should I do before going into the mines?

Make sure to take your troops with you because there are monsters in the mine. Also, ensure you have enough resources to sustain your expedition.
