About Goods Master

Goods Master is a relaxing sorting puzzle that turns store tidying into a satisfying challenge. Your goal is to clear shelves by grouping matching goods—jars, bottles, and boxes—into complete sets. Each cleared shelf makes room for the next batch, but you must work within a limited number of moves per level.

Master the Move Limit

In Goods Master, you pick up a good and place it into an empty slot on a shelf. When a shelf holds only matching items, it clears away, freeing space for new goods to arrive. The core loop is simple: assess the scattered items, plan your placements, and execute with precision to avoid wasting moves.

Each level gives you a finite number of moves, so every tap counts. As you progress through over 20 hand-crafted levels, the difficulty ramps up with more complex arrangements and tighter move budgets. When you're stuck, boosters like shuffle, transform, and merge can turn the tide, while colorful cartoon art and combo effects keep the experience light and rewarding.

PLAY BETTER

Goods Master tips

  1. Plan several moves ahead to avoid wasting moves on unnecessary placements.
  2. Use the shuffle booster when you have no obvious matches to break a deadlock.
  3. Transform a good to match a nearly complete shelf instead of moving many items.
  4. Save the hammer merge for when you have two items that can combine into a needed good.
QUICK ANSWERS

Goods Master FAQ

How do I clear a shelf in Goods Master?

Fill a shelf with goods that all match in type—like all jars or all bottles. Once a shelf contains only matching items, it clears automatically and makes room for new goods.

What happens if I run out of moves?

If you run out of moves before clearing all shelves, the level ends. You'll need to restart or use boosters earlier to succeed within the move limit.

What do the boosters do in Goods Master?

The shuffle booster rearranges items on the board, the transform booster changes one good into another type, and the hammer tool merges items together. Use them strategically when you're stuck.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by observing the shelves and the goods available. Tap or click a good to pick it up, then tap an empty shelf slot to place it. Your goal is to fill a shelf with matching items to clear it. Keep an eye on your move counter—each placement uses one move. If you're stuck, use boosters at the bottom: shuffle to rearrange items, transform to change one good into another, or merge to combine items with the hammer tool. Complete all shelves to finish the level.
