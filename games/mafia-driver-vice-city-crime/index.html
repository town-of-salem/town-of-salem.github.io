About Mafia Driver Vice City Crime

Mafia Driver Vice City Crime puts you behind the wheel of a returning convict in a big Italian city. Your goal is to rebuild your criminal standing from the bottom up. Drive through the streets, complete missions, and prove you still have what it takes to lead.

Driving Your Way to the Top

The core gameplay revolves around driving through the city in a 3D environment. You'll use WASD to control your vehicle and switch camera angles with C to get the best view. As you navigate the streets, you'll take on tasks that help you regain your position in the mob hierarchy.

Progress isn't handed to you; you'll need to earn respect through your driving skills and decision-making. The city is full of opportunities and dangers, and every mission tests your ability to handle the wheel under pressure. Climbing the ranks requires patience and nerve, making each success feel earned.

PLAY BETTER

Mafia Driver Vice City Crime tips

  1. Use the C key to switch camera angles for better visibility in tight spots.
  2. Master the WASD controls to handle sharp turns and avoid crashes.
  3. Prioritize completing missions that offer the most reputation gain.
  4. Keep an eye on your surroundings to avoid police or rival gang members.
  5. Take breaks with Esc if you need to plan your next move.
QUICK ANSWERS

Mafia Driver Vice City Crime FAQ

What is the main objective in Mafia Driver Vice City Crime?

You play a gangster just out of jail who must reclaim his position in the mob. You start from the bottom and work your way up by driving and completing missions.

How do I control the car in Mafia Driver Vice City Crime?

Use the WASD keys to drive. Press C to change the camera view and Esc to pause the game.

Is Mafia Driver Vice City Crime a racing game or a simulator?

It's a driving simulator set in a city, with a focus on crime and progression. You drive around to complete objectives and rise in the mob hierarchy.
