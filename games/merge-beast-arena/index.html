Merge Beast Arena

Merge Beast Arena is an idle merge game and auto-battler where you tap animals for coins and merge identical ones to evolve them into mightier beasts.

About Merge Beast Arena

Merge Beast Arena blends idle tapping with auto-battling. You start with a tiny mouse and tap it to earn coins. Drag two identical animals together to merge them into a bigger, stronger beast, climbing a 19-tier food chain from mouse to dragon.

Master the Merge Chain

The core loop is simple: tap your current beast to collect coins, then drag a duplicate onto it to trigger a merge. Each fusion produces the next animal in the ladder—rabbit, cat, fox, wolf, and so on—with each tier visibly larger and paying more per tap. Build a tap combo up to x5 for a significant coin boost.

As your beasts grow, so does the challenge. Merging two dragons awakens the magma boss, a tier-20 monster that one-shots anything it touches. Your decisions about when to merge and how fast to tap shape your progress, pushing you to balance immediate gains with long-term evolution.

PLAY BETTER

Merge Beast Arena tips

  1. Tap rapidly to reach the x5 combo multiplier for maximum coin earnings.
  2. Always merge identical animals as soon as possible to unlock higher-tier beasts.
  3. Focus on upgrading to bigger beasts to increase your coins per tap.
  4. Save up to merge two dragons to awaken the magma boss for a massive power spike.
QUICK ANSWERS

Merge Beast Arena FAQ

How do I merge animals in Merge Beast Arena?

Drag one animal on top of another of the same kind. They will fuse into the next beast in the evolution chain.

What is the highest tier beast in Merge Beast Arena?

The magma boss is a tier-20 monster that you can awaken by merging two dragons. It one-shots anything it touches.

How can I earn coins faster in Merge Beast Arena?

Tap your animals rapidly to build a combo multiplier up to x5. Bigger beasts also pay more per tap, so merging to higher tiers increases your coin income.
