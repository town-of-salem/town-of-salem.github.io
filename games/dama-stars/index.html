Dama Stars

Dama Stars is an Italian checkers game where you jump opponents' pieces, promote to kings, and play solo or with a friend.

About Dama Stars

Dama Stars brings the classic Italian checkers board to your screen with a fresh twist. Jump over your opponent's pieces to capture them, and guide your pieces to the far side to promote them into powerful kings. Play solo against the computer or challenge a friend in a head-to-head match.

Master the Board with Kings

In Dama Stars, each move matters as you slide your pieces diagonally across the board. Capture your opponent's pieces by jumping over them, and aim to reach the last row to crown your pieces as kings. Kings move more freely, giving you a strategic edge. The game offers three difficulty levels, so you can start easy and ramp up the challenge as you improve.

Whether you're playing solo against the computer or facing a friend, every match tests your foresight and planning. Choose the best move from several options, set up multi-jump chains, and protect your kings from being captured. With its family-friendly design and brain-teasing puzzles, Dama Stars is a great way to sharpen your tactical thinking.

PLAY BETTER

Dama Stars tips

  1. Plan multi-jump sequences to capture several pieces in one turn.
  2. Keep your back row intact as long as possible to prevent early king promotions.
  3. Use your kings to control the center of the board and limit your opponent's moves.
  4. Sacrifice a piece if it sets up a stronger position or a double jump.
  5. Study your opponent's moves and anticipate their next jump to avoid traps.
QUICK ANSWERS

Dama Stars FAQ

How do I play Dama Stars?

Click or tap a piece to select it, then choose a highlighted square to move. Jump over opponent pieces to capture them. Reach the last row to promote your piece to a king.

Can I play Dama Stars with a friend?

Yes, Dama Stars includes a two-player mode where you can play head-to-head on the same device.

What are the difficulty levels in Dama Stars?

There are three difficulty levels, allowing you to choose a challenge that matches your experience, from beginner to advanced.
