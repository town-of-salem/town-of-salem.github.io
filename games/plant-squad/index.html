About Plant Squad

Plant Squad is a classic tower defense game where you must save the world from a zombie takeover. Your mission is to combine plants to create more powerful versions, then place them strategically to defend against waves of zombies. As the battle intensifies, you'll need to adapt your tactics to survive.

Mastering Plant Fusion

The core gameplay loop in Plant Squad revolves around merging plants to unlock stronger variants. You'll start with basic plants and combine them to form more powerful defenders, each with unique abilities. Positioning these plants along the path is crucial, as you must create a layered defense that can handle zombies coming from multiple directions.

As you progress, the zombie horde becomes more formidable, forcing you to think ahead and optimize your plant combinations. The challenge lies in balancing offense and defense, deciding when to invest in new plants versus upgrading existing ones. With each wave, you'll need to refine your strategy to ensure no zombie breaks through your line.

PLAY BETTER

Plant Squad tips

  1. Combine plants early to create powerful defenders before the zombie horde arrives.
  2. Place plants strategically to cover all approach paths, not just the main lane.
  3. Upgrade your plants regularly to keep up with the increasing zombie strength.
  4. Don't neglect basic plants; they can be useful as cheap filler while you build up resources.
QUICK ANSWERS

Plant Squad FAQ

What is the main objective in Plant Squad?

The main objective is to defend against waves of zombies by combining plants to create stronger defenders and placing them strategically to eliminate all zombies.

How do I combine plants in Plant Squad?

To combine plants, you need to have two or more of the same type. Select one and then click on another identical plant to merge them into a more powerful version.

What controls are used in Plant Squad?

Plant Squad is controlled using the mouse or touch. You click or tap to select and place plants on the battlefield.
