About Ghost Town Escape 4 – Mirrored Dimension

Ghost Town Escape 4 – Mirrored Dimension is a point-and-click puzzle adventure set in an abandoned military town. You're called to assist spectral residents by collecting 45 mysterious coins. These coins activate an exit portal, but eerie whispers guide your mission as you seek relics tied to spirits' memories.

Collect Coins and Relics

Your main task is to explore the ghost town, searching for 45 mysterious coins hidden throughout the environment. As you click on objects and locations, you'll uncover relics linked to the spirits' memories. Each relic you find brings you closer to earning their appreciation, which is key to unlocking the exit portal.

The challenge lies in solving spectral enigmas and overcoming obstacles that block your path. The whispers you hear are not just atmosphere—they offer subtle guidance. You must decide which areas to investigate first and how to use the relics effectively. The journey blends physical exploration with ethereal puzzles, testing your observation and logic.

PLAY BETTER

Ghost Town Escape 4 – Mirrored Dimension tips

  1. Listen carefully to the whispers—they often point toward hidden coins or relics.
  2. Examine every object you find; some may be usable or combine with others.
  3. If stuck, try clicking on different parts of the screen to reveal hidden hotspots.
  4. Prioritize finding relics early, as they may unlock areas with more coins.

Ghost Town Escape 4 – Mirrored Dimension FAQ

What is the main objective in Ghost Town Escape 4 – Mirrored Dimension?

Your goal is to gather 45 mysterious coins to activate an exit portal. Along the way, you'll also find relics tied to spirits' memories, which are necessary to progress.

How do I control the game?

On PC, use your mouse to click on objects and locations. On mobile devices, simply tap the screen to interact. The game is fully playable with either input method.

What role do the whispers play in the game?

The eerie whispers serve as guidance, offering hints about where to find coins, relics, or how to solve puzzles. Paying attention to them can help you navigate the ghost town more effectively.
