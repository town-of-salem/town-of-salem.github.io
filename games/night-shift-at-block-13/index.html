About Night Shift at Block 13

In Night Shift at Block 13, you are the night guard of a crumbling Soviet-era apartment block where something is very wrong. Each night, visitors appear at your window claiming to be residents. Some are telling the truth, but others are shapeshifters wearing stolen faces. Your job is to survive all 7 nights by making the right call on who to admit.

Reading Visitors and Making Calls

Your core loop is simple: a visitor appears at the window, and you must decide whether to let them in or deny them. To make that call, you inspect items like passports, journals, phones, keys, letters, books, and receipts. These clues help you verify whether the person matches the resident they claim to be.

The tension builds because one wrong decision—letting a shapeshifter in or turning away a real resident—ends the night, and the building remembers. You must balance logic and observation under pressure. With 7 nights to survive, you'll need to learn from each mistake and refine your judgment to prove you belong in the block.

PLAY BETTER

Night Shift at Block 13 tips

  1. Check documents for mismatched names, dates, or photos that might reveal a shapeshifter.
  2. Use the journal and phone to cross-reference details about residents.
  3. Pay attention to small inconsistencies in letters or receipts that could be clues.
  4. If you're unsure, err on the side of caution—denying a real resident is costly, but letting in a shapeshifter is fatal.
  5. Learn from each night; the building remembers your mistakes, so adapt your strategy.

Night Shift at Block 13 FAQ

What is the objective in Night Shift at Block 13?

You must survive all 7 nights as the night guard of Block 13. Each night, visitors come to your window claiming to be residents. You need to decide whether to let them in or deny them based on evidence, avoiding fatal mistakes.

How do I know if a visitor is a shapeshifter?

Inspect items like passports, journals, phones, keys, letters, books, and receipts for inconsistencies. Shapeshifters may have stolen faces, so look for mismatched details that don't align with the real resident's information.

What happens if I make a wrong decision?

If you let a shapeshifter in or turn away a real resident, the night ends immediately. The building remembers your mistake, so you'll need to start over and try again with better judgment.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by moving around your guard station with WASD and looking with the mouse. When a visitor arrives, press E to interact with items they provide or that you have on hand. Inspect passports, journals, phones, keys, letters, books, and receipts for inconsistencies. Once you've gathered enough evidence, use the Green Button to let the visitor in or the Red Button to deny them. Survive each night and repeat for 7 nights.
