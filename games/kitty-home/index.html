About Kitty Home

Kitty Home is a delightful puzzle adventure where you draw a line to create a safe path for adorable kittens. Your main objective is to guide every kitty to the correct home, avoiding obstacles and traps along the way. With each level, the challenges become more engaging, testing your logic and creativity.

Drawing the Safest Path

In Kitty Home, your primary action is drawing a line to form a path for the kittens. You must carefully plan the route to avoid obstacles, traps, and collisions. Each level presents a new layout, requiring you to think ahead and adjust your drawing to ensure every kitty reaches its designated home safely.

As you progress, the puzzles become more complex with moving hazards and tricky layouts. You'll need to find the shortest and smartest route to complete each level and unlock new challenges. The game encourages quick thinking and creative problem-solving, making each success satisfying and rewarding.

PLAY BETTER

Kitty Home tips

  1. Plan your route before drawing to avoid obstacles.
  2. Watch for moving hazards and time your drawing accordingly.
  3. Try to find the shortest path to save time and avoid risks.
  4. If a level seems impossible, look for alternative routes or use the environment to your advantage.

Kitty Home FAQ

What is the objective in Kitty Home?

The goal is to draw a line to create a safe path for each kitten, guiding them to their correct home while avoiding obstacles and traps.

How do you control the game?

You use your mouse or touch to draw a line on the screen, which becomes the path the kittens follow.

Are there different challenges as you progress?

Yes, later levels introduce moving hazards and more complex layouts, requiring careful planning and quick thinking to complete.
