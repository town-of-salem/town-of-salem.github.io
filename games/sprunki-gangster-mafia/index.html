About Sprunki Gangster Mafia

Sprunki Gangster Mafia is an action-packed shooting game set in the neon-lit streets of Las Vegas. You play as a ruthless Sprunki, armed with a vast array of weapons and exotic cars. Your goal: do whatever it takes to rake in a vast fortune and become a billionaire in a lawless city.

Rise to Riches in Vegas

The core loop revolves around engaging in intense shootouts, completing daring missions, and participating in high-speed street races. Each successful heist or race earns you valuable loot, which you can use to upgrade your arsenal and customize your ride. The city is your playground, but every corner hides danger.

As you accumulate wealth, you'll face tougher enemies and riskier jobs. The world has no law, so you must be ready to defend yourself at the drop of a hat. Your decisions—whether to go in guns blazing or plan a stealthy approach—shape your path to the top. The thrill of outgunning rivals and outrunning the cops keeps the adrenaline high.

PLAY BETTER

Sprunki Gangster Mafia tips

  1. Prioritize upgrading your weapons early to handle tougher enemies.
  2. Use cover during shootouts to minimize damage.
  3. In races, learn the shortcuts to gain an edge.
  4. Collect loot from every mission to fund your rise to billionaire status.
  5. Stay alert—enemies can ambush you at any moment.
QUICK ANSWERS

Sprunki Gangster Mafia FAQ

What is the main objective in Sprunki Gangster Mafia?

The main objective is to become a billionaire by completing missions, winning races, and collecting loot in the lawless streets of Las Vegas.

How do I control my character in Sprunki Gangster Mafia?

The game is controlled by tapping on the screen—tap to shoot and tap to drive. The controls are simple and responsive.

What kind of gameplay can I expect?

Expect intense shootouts, daring missions, and high-speed street races. You'll need to defend yourself at all times while building your fortune.
