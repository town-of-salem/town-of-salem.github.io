About My Cat Town

My Cat Town invites you into a charming world of cats, blending doll placement with exploration. With 8 scenes like a train station, a cat's home, and a flower garden, you'll satisfy your curiosity about feline life through interactive play.

Explore Eight Cat-Themed Scenes

In My Cat Town, you'll move through a series of beautifully illustrated scenes, each offering a unique slice of cat life. From the bustling train station to the serene flower garden, you can click or tap to interact with the environment, placing dolls and discovering the little details that make each location special. The core loop is simple: explore, interact, and enjoy the whimsical atmosphere.

The game's challenge lies in uncovering all the hidden interactions within each scene. As you progress, you'll learn how different elements respond to your clicks, encouraging you to experiment and observe. The variety of scenes keeps the experience fresh, and the gentle pace makes it perfect for casual play. Whether you're a cat lover or just curious, My Cat Town offers a relaxing yet engaging way to spend your time.
