About Supercars Garage

Supercars Garage is an idle merge racing game where you collect and upgrade supercars. Buy cars, place them on the track, and watch them earn money as they race. Merge two cars of the same level to unlock faster, more valuable vehicles and expand your garage.

Merge and Upgrade Your Fleet

The core loop is simple: purchase cars, put them on the track, and they generate income over time. When you have two cars of the same level, merge them to create a single, more powerful vehicle. This not only frees up space but also increases your earning potential, letting you afford even better supercars.

As you progress, you'll discover new supercars and face the challenge of balancing your budget between buying new cars and upgrading your garage. Strategic merging is key—decide which cars to combine for maximum profit. The idle mechanics mean your cars keep racing even when you're away, so you can return to a growing bank balance.

PLAY BETTER

Supercars Garage tips

  1. Prioritize merging cars of the same level to free up track space and boost income.
  2. Save money for higher-level cars instead of buying many low-level ones.
  3. Upgrade your garage regularly to increase the earning rate of all cars.
  4. Keep an eye on your income to know when you can afford the next purchase.
QUICK ANSWERS

Supercars Garage FAQ

How do I merge cars in Supercars Garage?

To merge, you need two cars of the same level. Click and drag one car onto the other to combine them into a single, higher-level car.

What is the main objective in Supercars Garage?

The goal is to build the ultimate supercar collection by buying, merging, and upgrading cars to increase your income and unlock new vehicles.

How do I earn money in Supercars Garage?

Cars placed on the track earn money automatically as they race. The higher the car's level, the more money it generates per second.
