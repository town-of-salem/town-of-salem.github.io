About Zuma Boom 2

Zuma Boom 2 is a puzzle game where you shoot colored balls to form matches of three or more and clear the chain before it reaches the finish hole. The sequel introduces new challenges and bonuses to keep the classic arcade action fresh.

Mastering Chain Reactions

The core loop is simple: aim your gun at the rolling line of balls and fire to create groups of three or more matching colors. When a match forms, those balls disappear, and any balls behind them that are no longer connected to the front of the line will also drop away. This can trigger chain reactions that clear large sections and rack up bonus points.

The sequel adds armored balls that require two matches to destroy, forcing you to plan your shots more carefully. Bridges let the line cross over itself, creating new angles and obstacles. The new rainbow ball acts as a wildcard, matching any color and helping you out of tight spots. With 15 new levels, the challenge escalates as you learn to use these tools effectively.

PLAY BETTER

Zuma Boom 2 tips

  1. Plan shots to create chain reactions by targeting the back of a group of the same color.
  2. Use the rainbow ball strategically to match any color and clear a stubborn section.
  3. Watch for armored balls and hit them twice to break them, but don't ignore the rest of the line.
  4. When the line gets close to the hole, prioritize clearing the front balls to buy time.
QUICK ANSWERS

Zuma Boom 2 FAQ

What is the goal in Zuma Boom 2?

The goal is to clear the chain of colored balls by matching three or more of the same color before it reaches the finish hole.

How do I swap the ball I'm shooting?

Click the gun or press Space on desktop to swap the current ball with the next one in the queue.

What are the new features in Zuma Boom 2?

The sequel adds 15 new levels, armored balls that need two matches, bridges that let the line cross itself, and a new rainbow ball bonus.
