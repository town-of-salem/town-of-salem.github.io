About MATCH NONSTOP

MATCH NONSTOP puts a fresh spin on classic mahjong solitaire. Your goal is simple: tap tiles to move them into the bottom slots, and when three identical tiles land there, they vanish. Clear every tile from the board to win, but watch out—tiles are stacked in tricky positions that require careful observation.

Mastering the Tile Stack

The core loop revolves around spotting matching tiles among the stacks. Each tap sends a tile to one of the bottom slots, and three of the same kind automatically disappear. Since tiles are layered, you must plan which ones to remove first to avoid getting stuck with no available matches.

As the board gets more complex, your observation skills become crucial. When you run into a dead end, helper tools like shuffle and undo can save the day. Use them wisely to rearrange the layout or revert a mistake, then push forward to clear all tiles and win the puzzle.

PLAY BETTER

MATCH NONSTOP tips

  1. Always prioritize tiles that are free on top of stacks to keep options open.
  2. Before tapping, check if the tile you need is buried under others—plan your moves ahead.
  3. Use shuffle when no matches are available to avoid wasting moves.
  4. Undo can help you recover from a misclick that blocks your progress.
QUICK ANSWERS

MATCH NONSTOP FAQ

How do you win in MATCH NONSTOP?

Clear all tiles from the board by matching three identical tiles in the bottom slots. Once every tile is removed, you win the puzzle.

What happens when three identical tiles are placed in the bottom slots?

They are automatically removed from the game, freeing up space in the slots for more tiles.

What are the helper tools in MATCH NONSTOP?

The game provides shuffle and undo tools. Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles, while undo lets you revert your last move to correct mistakes.
