About Dinosaur Dig

Dinosaur Dig invites you to step into the boots of a paleontologist on a quest to uncover ancient fossils. Travel across a map, choose excavation sites, and dig through layers of soil to find hidden bones. Clean each discovery carefully and assemble complete dinosaur skeletons piece by piece.

Excavate and Assemble Skeletons

Each stage in Dinosaur Dig presents a unique activity, from digging and using tools to locating bone fragments and cleaning them. After uncovering the pieces, you drag them into the correct positions to reconstruct the skeleton. The variety keeps the gameplay fresh and engaging.

As you progress, you unlock new excavation sites on the map, offering more discoveries and challenges. The cheerful mini-games and puzzles add a playful twist, making the experience suitable for children and dinosaur enthusiasts alike. It's a relaxing, educational journey through prehistoric times.

PLAY BETTER

Dinosaur Dig tips

  1. Work slowly when removing soil to avoid damaging bones
  2. Clean each fossil thoroughly before attempting assembly
  3. Observe the skeleton outline to place pieces correctly
  4. Revisit earlier sites to practice and improve your technique
QUICK ANSWERS

Dinosaur Dig FAQ

What is the main objective in Dinosaur Dig?

The main objective is to excavate fossils from different sites, clean them, and assemble complete dinosaur skeletons by dragging pieces into place.

How do I control the game?

You use a mouse or touchscreen to select locations, operate tools, remove soil, clean fossils, and drag bone pieces to assemble skeletons.

Is Dinosaur Dig suitable for children?

Yes, the game is designed for children and anyone who enjoys dinosaurs, discovery, and relaxing educational adventures.

Once you have all the parts, drag them into the correct places to assemble the complete skeleton. Complete the activity to unlock new sites and continue your adventure.
