About Ghost Town Escape 3 – Mirrored Dimension

Ghost Town Escape 3 – Mirrored Dimension drops you into a desolate ghost town where your only goal is to find 35 mystical relics and activate the portal to escape. This point-and-click adventure blends exploration with puzzle-solving, challenging you to observe your surroundings and piece together clues in a hauntingly empty world.

Uncovering the 35 Relics

Your journey begins in a forsaken necropolis, where you must click and tap to interact with objects, examine nooks, and solve environmental puzzles. Each relic you find brings you closer to unlocking the dormant portal, but the path is not straightforward. You'll need to combine items, manipulate switches, and decipher cryptic clues scattered across the ghost town.

The challenge lies in the mirrored dimension's twisted logic, where familiar objects may hold hidden purposes. As you progress, the puzzles grow more intricate, testing your observation and deductive skills. With 35 relics to collect, patience and thorough exploration are key. Every corner could hide a clue, and every decision might be the one that leads you to freedom.

Ghost Town Escape 3 – Mirrored Dimension FAQ

What is the main objective in Ghost Town Escape 3 – Mirrored Dimension?

Your goal is to find 35 mystical relics hidden throughout the ghost town. Once all relics are collected, the portal to escape will activate.

How do I control the game?

You use your mouse to click on objects and navigate the environment. On touchscreen devices, simply tap to interact.

What type of puzzles can I expect?

Expect point-and-click puzzles that involve finding hidden objects, combining items, and solving environmental riddles. The mirrored dimension adds a twist, so think creatively.
