About Obby: Lucky Block Heist

Obby: Lucky Block Heist is a 3D multiplayer obstacle game where you build a base, collect rare Brainrot items, and sneak into guarded zones to steal legendary Lucky Blocks. Your goal is to grow your empire by upgrading stats and using the rebirth system to unlock powerful abilities.

Steal, Build, and Upgrade

In Obby: Lucky Block Heist, you start by exploring dangerous zones to find Brainrot items. These items generate passive income when placed in your base, so you'll want to grab as many as you can. But the real prize is the legendary Lucky Blocks hidden in heavily guarded areas. Sneak past patrolling enemies to snatch them, but if you're caught, you'll be blasted back to the start.

The challenge ramps up as you face tougher guards and more complex layouts. Upgrade your speed and jump height to outmaneuver enemies, and use the rebirth system to unlock multi-jumps that let you reach new heights. Each heist is a risk-reward decision: do you play it safe with Brainrot or go for the big score? Your choices determine how fast you become the richest player.

PLAY BETTER

Obby: Lucky Block Heist tips

  1. Prioritize collecting Brainrot early to build a steady income stream.
  2. Scout guard patrol routes before attempting a heist to avoid being caught.
  3. Upgrade jump height first to reach high-value areas that are easier to defend.
  4. Use the rebirth system strategically to unlock multi-jumps for late-game zones.
  5. Sell Brainrot when you have a surplus to fund upgrades without slowing your progress.
QUICK ANSWERS

Obby: Lucky Block Heist FAQ

What is the main objective in Obby: Lucky Block Heist?

The main objective is to build a base, collect Brainrot items for passive income, and steal legendary Lucky Blocks from guarded zones. Upgrading your stats and using rebirths helps you become the richest player.

How do I avoid getting caught by guards?

Watch guard patrol patterns, move carefully, and use your speed and jump upgrades to outmaneuver them. If caught, you'll be blasted back, so plan your route and time your moves.

What does the rebirth system do?

The rebirth system lets you reset your progress to unlock multi-jumps, which allow you to reach higher areas and access better loot. It's a key progression mechanic for late-game success.
