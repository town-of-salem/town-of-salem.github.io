About PieceAlive

PieceAlive is a relaxing visual puzzle adventure that invites you to restore stunning scenes piece by piece. Choose a world, pick a difficulty, and study the picture before dragging every piece into its correct position. Complete scenes to bring them to life and unlock new locations.

Master Every Scene

In PieceAlive, each puzzle begins with a scattered set of pieces. You can zoom in to examine details, use hints to place a stubborn piece, and drag elements into place with your mouse or finger. The core loop is simple: select a scene, choose Easy, Moderate, or Hard, and rebuild the image until it matches the reference.

As you complete scenes, you earn mastery stars and coins, which help unlock new worlds and locations. With 50 scenes featuring landmarks, wildlife, and nature, the challenge scales with difficulty. A fresh Daily Challenge keeps the experience varied, encouraging you to return and test your skills across all levels.

PLAY BETTER

PieceAlive tips

  1. Start with Easy puzzles to learn the drag mechanics and build confidence.
  2. Use the zoom function to inspect fine details that help match pieces.
  3. Save hints for Moderate or Hard puzzles where pieces are more challenging.
  4. Complete scenes on all difficulties to earn more mastery stars and coins.
  5. Check back daily for the fresh Daily Challenge to test your skills.
QUICK ANSWERS

PieceAlive FAQ

How does PieceAlive work?

PieceAlive is a jigsaw puzzle game where you rebuild scenes by dragging pieces into place. Choose a world, select a difficulty, and complete the picture to earn stars and coins.

What controls are used in PieceAlive?

You can use a mouse to click and drag pieces, or touch and drag on mobile devices. There are also zoom buttons and a hint button, and pressing H activates a hint.

How do I unlock new locations in PieceAlive?

Complete scenes to earn mastery stars and coins. These rewards help unlock new locations as you progress through the game.
