About Mafia Wars

Mafia Wars puts you in charge of a small criminal group fighting for survival against rival mafia factions. As the leader, your goal is to protect your crew and carve out your place in a hostile underworld. Use strategy and firepower to outlast the competition.

Defending Your Turf

The core loop revolves around defending your position from waves of enemy attacks. You'll deploy weapons, lay mines, and toss grenades to repel invaders. Building barricades slows their advance, giving you precious time to pick them off. Recruiting new members strengthens your gang, ensuring you have enough firepower to hold the line.

Every encounter tests your tactical decisions. Do you focus on fortifying your defenses or go on the offensive? Managing your resources and crew is key to surviving the escalating pressure. As the leader, your choices determine whether your gang thrives or gets wiped out. Expect intense, fast-paced action that rewards quick thinking and adaptability.

PLAY BETTER

Mafia Wars tips

  1. Use barricades to slow enemy advances and create firing lanes.
  2. Save grenades for groups of enemies to maximize damage.
  3. Recruit new members early to build a stronger defense.
  4. Keep moving to avoid being surrounded.
QUICK ANSWERS

Mafia Wars FAQ

What is the main objective in Mafia Wars?

Your goal is to lead a small criminal group and protect your crew from rival mafia attacks. You must survive by using weapons, mines, grenades, and barricades while recruiting new members.

How do I control my character in Mafia Wars?

You use your mouse or touch screen to aim and shoot. Click or tap to deploy items like mines and grenades, and drag to place barricades.

What strategies work best for surviving in Mafia Wars?

Focus on building strong defenses with barricades and using area-of-effect weapons like grenades on clustered enemies. Recruiting new members early gives you more firepower to withstand attacks.
