About Dinosaur Park Jurassic Dino World

Dinosaur Park Jurassic Dino World drops you into the role of a powerful wild dinosaur fighting to survive on a mysterious island. Your goal is to hunt for food, gather resources, and use your instincts to outlast the giant predators that roam the dangerous prehistoric jungle.

Surviving the Prehistoric Jungle

The core loop revolves around exploring the island to find prey and resources. Use your attacks to take down food, and keep an eye on your surroundings for threats. Sprinting helps you close distance or escape, while your roar can intimidate rivals. Special moves, including a rewarded-ad-powered attack, give you an edge in tough fights.

Survival demands smart decisions. You must balance hunting with resource gathering, and know when to fight or flee. Some levels offer unique special actions that can turn the tide. As you progress, you'll face increasingly dangerous predators, testing your reflexes and strategic use of your abilities.

PLAY BETTER

Dinosaur Park Jurassic Dino World tips

  1. Use sprint to close distance on prey or escape larger predators.
  2. Roar (E) can intimidate smaller dinosaurs, giving you a moment to act.
  3. Save your special attacks for tough fights or when outnumbered.
  4. Watch for the F key prompt in certain levels to trigger a unique action.
  5. Keep moving and use the camera to scan for threats before you commit to a hunt.

Dinosaur Park Jurassic Dino World FAQ

What is the main objective in Dinosaur Park Jurassic Dino World?

Your goal is to survive as a wild dinosaur on a mysterious island. You must hunt for food, gather resources, and defend yourself against giant predators to thrive in the prehistoric jungle.

How do I perform special attacks?

Special attacks are triggered with the V, C, or X keys. One of these may require watching a rewarded ad to activate. Use them strategically in combat for a powerful advantage.

Are there any level-specific actions?

Yes, some levels feature a special action bound to the F key. This action is only available in certain levels, so watch for on-screen prompts to use it when it appears.
