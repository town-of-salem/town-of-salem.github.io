About Tech Match

Tech Match is a computer-themed match-3 puzzle game that puts your quick thinking to the test. Your goal is to match three or more identical tech items, like CPUs, keyboards, and mice, to rack up points and progress to the next level. With time as your only constraint, every move counts.

Beat the Clock

In Tech Match, the core loop is simple: scan the grid for clusters of identical tech components, then chain three or more together to clear them and earn points. Each successful match boosts your score and brings you closer to the level goal. The faster you spot and create matches, the more points you can accumulate before time runs out.

Time is the ultimate pressure in Tech Match. As you advance, the challenge intensifies, forcing you to make quicker decisions and prioritize efficient matches. Your score directly influences your progression, so strategic planning and rapid pattern recognition become essential. It's a test of both speed and puzzle-solving skill.

PLAY BETTER

Tech Match tips

  1. Look for larger clusters first to maximize your score per move.
  2. Plan your matches to create cascading chains, clearing more items at once.
  3. Prioritize matches near the bottom to potentially shift the grid and reveal new opportunities.
  4. Keep a mental note of the timer and balance speed with accuracy.

Tech Match FAQ

How do you play Tech Match?

In Tech Match, you match three or more identical tech items, such as CPUs, keyboards, and mice, by clicking or tapping them. Each match increases your score and helps you advance to the next level.

What is the main objective in Tech Match?

The main objective is to score as many points as possible by matching tech items before time runs out. Reaching the level's score target advances you to the next level.

What limits your play in Tech Match?

Time is the only limiting factor. You must make matches quickly to accumulate enough points before the timer expires.
