About Deep Drill

Deep Drill is a mining idle game that plays itself from the moment the page loads. There is no home screen or setup—the bit is already turning, and every meter it cuts pays ore. Your only job is to spend that ore on upgrades to make the well produce more, faster, and richer.

Twelve Rig Tracks to Master

The core loop is simple: the drill descends automatically, earning ore with each meter. You spend that ore on twelve rig tracks, each improving a different aspect of your operation. A bigger bit increases speed, coolant reduces rock hardness, and a wider hopper boosts richness. The game never stops, so every purchase pays off immediately.

There is no timer, no lives, and no way to lose—the well keeps producing even while you read the panel or close the tab. The night shift track makes coming back tomorrow worth more, rewarding patience and long-term planning. With a Buy x1 and Buy max mode, you can fine-tune your spending or blow the whole ladder at once.

PLAY BETTER

Deep Drill tips

  1. Use Buy max early to quickly climb the ladder and unlock higher tiers.
  2. Balance speed and coolant: faster drilling means more ore, but hardness slows you down.
  3. Invest in the night shift before you step away to maximize offline gains.
  4. Check the panel regularly to spend ore as soon as it accumulates—idle earnings are wasted if you don't reinvest.
QUICK ANSWERS

Deep Drill FAQ

How does Deep Drill make progress without input?

The drill descends automatically, and every meter cut pays ore. Even with no clicks, the well keeps producing, and it continues while the tab is closed, so you always earn.

What is the Buy x1 and Buy max mode for?

Buy x1 purchases a single level of an upgrade, while Buy max spends your entire ore balance on the highest affordable ladder of that upgrade, letting you quickly scale up.

Is there any way to lose in Deep Drill?

No. There is no timer, no lives, and no failure state. The well always produces, so you can play at your own pace without risk.

GUIDEHow to play＋
When you load Deep Drill, the drill is already working. Watch the ore counter climb as the bit descends. Use your mouse or touch to interact with the rig panel. Press a row's price key to buy one level of that upgrade. Switch between Buy x1 and Buy max mode to purchase a single level or spend your entire ore balance on the highest affordable ladder. Prioritize upgrades that match your current bottleneck—speed, hardness, or richness—and check back after the night shift to collect boosted earnings.
