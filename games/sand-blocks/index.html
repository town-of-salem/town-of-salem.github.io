About Sand Blocks

Sand Blocks is a logic puzzle that turns classic block-dropping into a flowing sand simulation. Your goal is to place colorful shapes on the grid, watch them break apart, and match same-colored sand to complete horizontal lines before the pile reaches the limit.

Master the Sand Flow

In Sand Blocks, you receive three block shapes at a time. You can place them in any order and anywhere on the grid. Each block shatters into sand that falls, stacks, and settles based on its shape, creating a dynamic pile that behaves differently with every drop.

The challenge is to plan ahead as sand accumulates unpredictably. Completing a full line of matching sand color clears space, but the game ends if sand rises past the limit line. Strategic placement and color matching are key to keeping the grid under control.

PLAY BETTER

Sand Blocks tips

  1. Plan the order of your three blocks to maximize line completions.
  2. Use the natural flow of sand to fill gaps and create same-color clusters.
  3. Prioritize clearing lines before the sand level gets too high.
  4. Think ahead about how each block shape will break and spread.

Sand Blocks FAQ

How does Sand Blocks work?

You drop block shapes that break into sand. The sand flows and settles based on the shape. Complete full horizontal lines of the same color to clear them and prevent the sand from reaching the limit line.

What are the controls for Sand Blocks?

Use the left mouse button to drag and drop blocks onto the grid. You can place them in any order and position.

What happens if the sand reaches the limit line?

The game ends when sand rises past the limit line. You need to keep the grid under control by clearing lines and planning your placements.
