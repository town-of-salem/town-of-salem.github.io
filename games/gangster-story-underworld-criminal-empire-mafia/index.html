About Gangster Story: Underworld Criminal Empire Mafia

Gangster Story: Underworld Criminal Empire Mafia drops you into a sin city where vendettas and turf wars are daily life. This open-world RPG crime simulator puts you behind the wheel of fast cars, racing through gangster chases while building your criminal empire in a mafia-run city.

Master the Turf War

Your core loop revolves around driving and fighting. Use WASD to steer through the city, hit Space to drift around corners, and chase down rivals in high-speed car pursuits. The open world lets you choose your route, whether you're escaping the law or hunting enemies.

Every decision affects your standing in the underworld. Turf wars demand strategic driving and quick reflexes, as you balance aggressive chases with smart escapes. Progression comes from surviving and outmaneuvering opponents, not just raw speed. The city of San Andreas is your battleground—dominate it or get crushed.

PLAY BETTER

Gangster Story: Underworld Criminal Empire Mafia tips

  1. Use Space to drift around sharp corners to maintain speed during chases.
  2. Keep an eye on your surroundings to avoid getting boxed in by rival cars.
  3. Practice drifting in open areas to master car control before engaging in turf wars.
  4. When escaping, use narrow streets to shake off pursuers.
QUICK ANSWERS

Gangster Story: Underworld Criminal Empire Mafia FAQ

What is the main objective in Gangster Story?

The main objective is to dominate the city through turf wars and car chases, building your criminal empire as you go.

How do I control the car?

Use WASD to drive and press Space to drift. This combo lets you handle sharp turns and high-speed chases.

Is the game an open-world RPG?

Yes, it's an open-world RPG where you explore a mafia-run city, engage in vendettas, and fight for control of territories.
