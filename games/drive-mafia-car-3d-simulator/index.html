About Drive Mafia Car 3D Simulator

Drive Mafia Car 3D Simulator is a 3D driving game that drops you into an open-world city with a sports car. Your goal is to drive freely, perform stunts, and drift at high speeds without worrying about traffic or police. The game's advanced physics engine makes every turn and slide feel realistic.

Mastering Drift and Burnout

The core loop is simple: accelerate, steer, and slide. You can perform illegal stunts like drifting and burnouts on the asphalt. Since there's no traffic or rival vehicles, you have the whole city to yourself. The physics engine responds to your inputs, making each drift feel weighty and satisfying.

The challenge comes from controlling your car at high speeds. You'll need to balance throttle and steering to keep your drift smooth. As you explore the open world, you'll find new spots to test your skills. The game rewards precision and timing, so practice makes perfect.

PLAY BETTER

Drive Mafia Car 3D Simulator tips

  1. Start with gentle turns to learn the car's weight transfer.
  2. Use the handbrake to initiate drifts in tight corners.
  3. Maintain throttle control to avoid spinning out.
  4. Explore the city to find wide open spaces for high-speed runs.

Drive Mafia Car 3D Simulator FAQ

What is the main objective in Drive Mafia Car 3D Simulator?

The main objective is to drive a sports car freely in an open-world city, performing stunts like drifting and burnouts without any traffic or police interference.

How do I control the car in Drive Mafia Car 3D Simulator?

You control the car using the keyboard. Use the arrow keys or WASD to steer and accelerate, and the spacebar for the handbrake if available.

Is there any traffic or police in the game?

No, the game is designed without traffic or police, so you can drive at full speed and perform illegal stunts without being chased.
