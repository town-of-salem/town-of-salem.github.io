The Wrong Ones

Read villagers’ statements, look for contradictions and identify the Skinwalker in a compact, mouse-driven deduction prototype.

Solo · deduction prototype

Play The Wrong Ones

Ready when you are.

Single-player prototype. Mouse controls. This is an early browser version rather than a large multiplayer campaign.

Claims are the shared starting point

The Wrong Ones asks you to examine what villagers say and find the account that does not fit. That is a familiar Salem problem: two people can sound equally plausible while their stories cannot both describe the same events. Treat each statement as a constraint, then look for a contradiction you can explain in one sentence.

How the puzzle differs from a live lobby

There is no team of human Town players to persuade. You inspect villager cards and accuse a suspect in a single-player scenario about a hidden Skinwalker. That removes chat speed and social pressure, leaving more room to examine the wording. It also changes the kind of evidence you should expect: apply the puzzle’s own rules instead of importing a Sheriff result or a Salem role immunity.

A useful first attempt

Read all available statements before committing to an accusation. Note who refers to whom, what event is described and whether a statement is a direct observation or an inference. Try a candidate explanation against the remaining cards. If it explains one discrepancy but creates two others, keep investigating. The prototype is best approached as a short exercise in checking testimony, with its current limitations in mind.
