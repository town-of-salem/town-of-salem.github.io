About Obby Tsunami Runner

Obby Tsunami Runner is a multiplayer browser game where you sprint from a relentless tsunami. Each wave brings new dangers, and your goal is to dodge obstacles, survive the flood, and earn wins to unlock characters and trails. Compete with friends and push your limits to see how far you can go.

Surviving the Wave

The core loop is simple: run forward as the tsunami chases you, jumping over obstacles and using WASD to steer. Holding the right mouse button lets you look around to spot hazards ahead. Every wave increases the challenge, so you must stay alert and react quickly to survive.

Winning races earns you rewards that unlock new characters and trails, including the legendary Tung-Tung-Tung Sahur. The multiplayer aspect means you're competing against friends and other players, adding pressure to perform. Each run tests your reflexes and decision-making, pushing you to improve your survival time.

PLAY BETTER

Obby Tsunami Runner tips

  1. Use the right mouse button to look ahead and plan your jumps early.
  2. Time your jumps carefully to clear obstacles without slowing down.
  3. Stay in the middle of the path to have more room to react.
  4. Keep an eye on the tsunami behind you to gauge your speed.
  5. Practice to learn obstacle patterns and improve your reaction time.
QUICK ANSWERS

Obby Tsunami Runner FAQ

How do you play Obby Tsunami Runner?

You run from a tsunami, using WASD to move, space to jump, and the right mouse button to look around. Your goal is to survive as long as possible and earn wins by finishing races.

What are the controls in Obby Tsunami Runner?

The controls are WASD to move, space to jump, and holding the right mouse button to look around. These let you navigate obstacles and stay ahead of the wave.

What can you unlock in Obby Tsunami Runner?

By earning wins, you can unlock new characters and trails. One of the characters you can play as is the legendary Tung-Tung-Tung Sahur.
