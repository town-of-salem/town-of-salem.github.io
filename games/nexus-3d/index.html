About Nexus 3D

Nexus 3D is a calming, glowing puzzle game that challenges you to connect all the neon wires and power the circuits. Your goal is to rotate nodes and create a continuous flow of energy from the power source to every target, with each level offering a fresh spatial challenge.

Rotating Nodes for Energy Flow

In Nexus 3D, each level presents a network of neon wires and nodes. You click or tap on a wire to rotate it, changing its orientation to link segments together. The core loop is simple: examine the layout, rotate pieces, and watch the energy flow as you complete the circuit from the source to all targets.

The game features an infinite number of procedurally generated levels, so the difficulty gradually increases as you progress. This means you'll constantly face new arrangements that require logical thinking and spatial planning. The relaxing atmosphere keeps the experience soothing, but the puzzles still demand careful decisions about which wires to rotate and in what order.

PLAY BETTER

Nexus 3D tips

  1. Look for the power source first and trace possible paths outward.
  2. Rotate wires one at a time and check how the flow changes.
  3. If a wire doesn't seem to fit, try rotating it in the opposite direction.
  4. Focus on connecting targets that are closest to the source first.
  5. Use the relaxing pace to plan several moves ahead without rushing.
QUICK ANSWERS

Nexus 3D FAQ

What is the objective in Nexus 3D?

Your goal is to rotate the neon wires to create a continuous flow of energy from the power source to all target nodes, powering the circuits.

How do I control the game?

You can rotate a wire by left-clicking with the mouse or tapping on it with your finger on a touch screen.

Are there many levels in Nexus 3D?

Yes, the game has an infinite number of procedurally generated levels, with difficulty gradually increasing as you progress.
