Snow Ball Race

Snow Ball Race is a winter racing game where you roll a snowball, collect snow to grow, and race to the finish line.

About Snow Ball Race

In Snow Ball Race, you take control of a snowball rolling down a snowy track. Your goal is to reach the finish line while collecting snow to make your ball bigger. The larger your snowball, the more impressive your run, but the track is full of obstacles that can slow you down or stop you.

Mastering the Roll

The core loop of Snow Ball Race is simple: roll forward, steer your snowball, and scoop up snow scattered along the track. Each piece of snow you collect increases your size, which can help you plow through smaller obstacles. But bigger isn't always better—a larger snowball can be harder to control, especially on tight turns.

As you progress, the levels become more challenging, with obstacles placed to test your timing and control. You'll need to choose the best path through each track, balancing the urge to grow your snowball against the risk of hitting a barrier. The game rewards careful planning and quick reactions, making every run a fresh test of your skills.

PLAY BETTER

Snow Ball Race tips

  1. Prioritize collecting snow early to build size, but don't take risky detours that could lead to crashes.
  2. Look ahead and plan your route to avoid obstacles while still grabbing snow piles.
  3. On tight turns, slow down slightly by steering less aggressively to maintain control.
  4. If you're stuck behind an obstacle, try a different path—sometimes the longer way is safer.
  5. Practice timing your clicks or taps to make precise adjustments.
QUICK ANSWERS

Snow Ball Race FAQ

What is the objective in Snow Ball Race?

The objective is to roll your snowball from the start to the finish line, collecting snow along the way to make it as large as possible while avoiding obstacles.

How do I control my snowball in Snow Ball Race?

You control the snowball with a mouse click or tap. Click or tap to steer left or right, guiding the snowball along the track.

What happens if I hit an obstacle in Snow Ball Race?

Hitting an obstacle can slow you down or stop your snowball, potentially costing you time and preventing you from reaching the finish line with a large snowball.
