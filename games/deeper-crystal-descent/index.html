About DEEPER! Crystal Descent

DEEPER! Crystal Descent is a strategic mining adventure where you pilot a drilling machine into an ever-expanding underground world. Your goal is to descend as deep as possible, collecting valuable resources along the way. Manage your energy and cargo wisely, then return to the surface to sell your haul and invest in permanent upgrades.

Plan Your Descent Route

Each expedition begins at the surface. You choose which connected route to take, weighing the potential rewards against the risks of energy consumption and cargo space. As you drill, you'll discover different resources scattered across mysterious underground biomes. The deeper you go, the richer the deposits become, but you must always keep enough energy to make it back.

Your drilling machine is your lifeline. Upgrade its drill, battery, cargo capacity, and drive system to push further and carry more. Every upgrade is permanent, so each trip makes you stronger. The tactical decisions you make about route and resource management determine how deep you can go. How far will you descend?

PLAY BETTER

DEEPER! Crystal Descent tips

  1. Always leave enough energy to return to the surface, or you'll lose your haul.
  2. Prioritize upgrades that match your playstyle: better drill for faster digging, larger cargo for more resources, or improved battery for longer trips.
  3. Explore different biomes to find a variety of resources, but don't stray too far from your return path.
  4. Plan your route to maximize resource collection while minimizing energy waste.
QUICK ANSWERS

DEEPER! Crystal Descent FAQ

What is the main objective in DEEPER! Crystal Descent?

The main objective is to drill as deep as possible, collecting valuable resources and upgrading your drilling machine to reach even greater depths.

How do I control the drilling machine?

You control the machine by tapping or clicking an available connected route to move. Use on-screen buttons to manage your expedition, return to the surface, sell resources, and buy upgrades.

What happens if I run out of energy while drilling?

If you run out of energy, you may not be able to return to the surface. It's crucial to manage your energy carefully and always keep enough to make the trip back.
