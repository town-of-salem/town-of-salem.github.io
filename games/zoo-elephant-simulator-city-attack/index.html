About Zoo Elephant Simulator City Attack

Zoo Elephant Simulator City Attack puts you in the role of a powerful wild elephant on a wildlife adventure. Explore forests and cities, raise your elephant family, and take on dangerous predators. With Jungle, City, and Free Mode, you'll always find a new challenge.

Mastering Elephant Combat and Survival

The core gameplay loop revolves around exploring vast environments, gathering resources, and engaging in combat. Use your elephant's strength to attack predators and complete action-packed missions. Sprint to escape danger or charge into battle, and interact with objects to progress through the world.

Each mode offers a different experience: Jungle focuses on survival, City brings urban chaos, and Free Mode lets you roam without objectives. As you play, you'll raise your elephant family, making decisions about when to fight and when to flee. The challenge escalates as predators become more aggressive and missions grow more complex.

PLAY BETTER

Zoo Elephant Simulator City Attack tips

  1. Use sprint to close distance quickly or escape from predators.
  2. Attack with E when enemies are in range, but time your strikes to avoid taking damage.
  3. Explore thoroughly to find resources and mission objectives.
  4. In Free Mode, practice combat and movement without pressure.
  5. Keep an eye on your elephant family and defend them from threats.
QUICK ANSWERS

Zoo Elephant Simulator City Attack FAQ

What are the three game modes in Zoo Elephant Simulator City Attack?

The game features Jungle, City, and Free Mode. Jungle and City offer structured missions, while Free Mode allows unrestricted exploration.

How do I control my elephant on mobile?

On mobile, use a joystick to move your elephant and drag the screen to rotate the camera. Actions like attacking are performed via on-screen buttons.

Can I pause the game?

Yes, you can pause the game at any time by pressing P on desktop or using the pause button in the UI.
