Mischievous Pairs

Mischievous Pairs is a memory card game where you flip cards to match pairs or triples, race against the clock, and unlock new levels.

About Mischievous Pairs

Mischievous Pairs puts your memory to the test as you flip cards and match pairs or triples to clear the board. With hundreds of levels and increasing difficulty, you'll need sharp recall and quick thinking to succeed. Play solo or challenge a friend in local two-player mode.

Master the Match

The core loop is simple: click or tap cards to reveal them, remember their positions, and find matching pairs or triples before time runs out. Each successful match clears the board and brings you closer to level completion. As you progress, the layouts become more complex, demanding better memory and faster decisions.

Beyond the base challenge, you can use coins to purchase boosts like Hints and Time Refills, which can turn the tide in tight spots. Coins are earned by playing, collecting free coins, or grabbing coin bonuses. In two-player mode, the pressure intensifies: you must find a match before a 10-second timer ends to keep your turn, while surprise Shuffle Time scrambles the board and keeps everyone on edge.

PLAY BETTER

Mischievous Pairs tips

  1. Focus on memorizing the positions of cards you've already flipped, not just the current one.
  2. Prioritize matching pairs or triples that are close together to save time.
  3. Save your coins for Hints when you're stuck on a difficult level.
  4. In two-player mode, plan your moves quickly to keep your turn and avoid giving your opponent a chance.
QUICK ANSWERS

Mischievous Pairs FAQ

How do I play Mischievous Pairs?

Flip cards by clicking or tapping them, remember their positions, and match pairs or triples to clear the board before time runs out. Use coins for boosts like Hints and Time Refills.

What happens in two-player mode?

In local two-player mode, you take turns flipping cards. If you find a match, you keep your turn, but you must do so before a 10-second timer ends. Surprise Shuffle Time can scramble the cards, adding extra challenge.

How do I unlock new levels?

Progress through the hundreds of levels by completing them. As you advance, the difficulty increases, and you'll unlock new levels to play.
