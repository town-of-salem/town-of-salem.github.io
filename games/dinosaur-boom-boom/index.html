About Dinosaur Boom Boom

Dinosaur Boom Boom is a fast-paced match-3 shooting game where you aim and shoot colorful dinosaur eggs to create matches of three or more. Your goal is to clear the moving chain of eggs before it reaches the end of the path. With simple mouse controls and vibrant visuals, every level tests your accuracy and strategic planning.

Master the Moving Chain

In Dinosaur Boom Boom, you shoot eggs from the bottom of the screen into a chain that continuously moves forward. When three or more eggs of the same color connect, they disappear, clearing space and preventing the chain from advancing too far. Careful aiming is essential, as each shot can either help you clear the chain or set up a powerful combo.

As you progress, the chain moves faster and the patterns become trickier, demanding quick thinking and precise shots. Triggering chain reactions by matching eggs that cause others to fall can earn you bonus points and clear larger sections at once. The challenge is to balance speed with accuracy, making every shot count to stop the chain and achieve the highest score.

PLAY BETTER

Dinosaur Boom Boom tips

  1. Plan your shots to create matches of three or more, but also look for chances to set up larger groups for bigger combos.
  2. Aim for eggs that are close to the end of the chain to buy yourself more time.
  3. Use wall bounces to reach eggs that are in awkward positions.
  4. Watch the chain's movement speed and adjust your aiming accordingly.
QUICK ANSWERS

Dinosaur Boom Boom FAQ

How do I play Dinosaur Boom Boom?

Use your mouse to aim and click to shoot eggs at the moving chain. Match three or more eggs of the same color to clear them and prevent the chain from reaching the end.

What is the main objective in Dinosaur Boom Boom?

The objective is to clear the entire chain of eggs before it reaches the end of the path. You do this by shooting eggs to create matches of three or more, which removes them from the chain.

Are there any special features like combos in Dinosaur Boom Boom?

Yes, you can trigger powerful combos and chain reactions by creating matches that cause other eggs to fall and match, which can clear larger sections and earn you more points.

To start playing Dinosaur Boom Boom, simply use your mouse to aim at the moving chain of eggs. Click to shoot the egg from the launcher at the bottom. Your goal is to hit the chain and create a group of three or more eggs of the same color. When they match, they disappear, and the chain shrinks. Keep shooting to clear all the eggs before the chain reaches the end. Watch for opportunities to set up combos by bouncing shots off walls or targeting clusters that will cause chain reactions.
