About WOMS World of Master Survivors

WOMS World of Master Survivors drops you into a fallen city where you are the final defense at a street corner. Your objective is to survive relentless zombie hordes, using the rewards from each wave to upgrade your firepower and tactical options.

Survive and Upgrade Between Waves

The core loop is straightforward: fight off endless waves of zombies, then spend your survival earnings on upgrades. You can purchase heavier ammo, deploy cover drones, set up turrets, or call in gunship strikes. Each choice shapes your strategy for the next assault, making every wave a test of both aim and resource management.

Challenge comes in two main modes: City Siege forces you to hold a narrow urban choke point, while Fortified Compound doubles the horde and gives you four corner turrets to manage. Daily Quests add three fresh objectives each day, rewarding smart play and keeping the action varied. Expect intense, top-down shooting with no pause for the undead.

PLAY BETTER

WOMS World of Master Survivors tips

  1. Keep moving to avoid getting cornered by the zombie horde.
  2. Save grenades for tight groups or when you're about to be overwhelmed.
  3. Deploy turrets in choke points to cover multiple angles.
  4. Call air support when a large wave is approaching to thin it out early.
  5. Prioritize upgrading your primary weapon before investing in drones.
QUICK ANSWERS

WOMS World of Master Survivors FAQ

What is the main objective in WOMS World of Master Survivors?

Your goal is to survive endless zombie waves while defending a street corner in a fallen city. You earn rewards from each wave to upgrade your weapons and deploy defensive tools.

How do I get upgrades in the game?

Surviving waves earns you currency that you spend between waves on upgrades like heavier ammo, cover drones, turrets, and gunship calls.

What are the different game modes?

There are two main modes: City Siege, which focuses on urban choke point survival, and Fortified Compound, which doubles the horde and provides four corner turrets. Daily Quests offer three fresh challenges each day.

GUIDEHow to play＋
Start by moving with WASD or arrow keys and aiming with your mouse. Left-click to fire at the incoming horde. As you survive waves, you'll earn currency to spend on upgrades between battles. Use Q to deploy a turret, E to call air support, and G to throw a grenade when the pressure builds. Keep moving to avoid being surrounded, and prioritize targets that threaten your position. On mobile, use the left thumb stick to move and the right button to fire, tapping on-screen buttons for special actions.
