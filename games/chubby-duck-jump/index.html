About Chubby Duck Jump

Chubby Duck Jump puts you in control of a chubby yellow duckling on a quest across an endless sky. The goal is simple: charge your jump and release to land on the next floating plank. Each successful landing scores a point, and the run continues until you misjudge a leap.

Mastering the Charge-and-Release Timing

The core loop revolves around a single button. Hold to charge a ring meter that shows your jump power, then release to launch the duck. The longer you hold, the longer the jump. Read the gap to the next plank and let go at the right moment to land safely. Charge too little and the duck splashes into the meadow below; hold too long and it overshoots into thin air.

Every landing adds a point, and each run is a chance to beat your personal best. Perfect streaks light up the sky, rewarding consistent timing. Runs are endless but last 60 seconds, so every second counts. With instant restart and one-hand play, you can quickly jump back in and refine your technique.

PLAY BETTER

Chubby Duck Jump tips

  1. Start with short holds to get a feel for the meter, then gradually increase power as gaps widen.
  2. Watch the ring meter carefully—it fills quickly, so release just before the desired power.
  3. Aim for the center of each plank to give yourself a safe margin for error.
  4. If you overshoot often, release slightly earlier; if you fall short, hold a bit longer.
  5. Stay relaxed and focus on rhythm rather than rushing each jump.
QUICK ANSWERS

Chubby Duck Jump FAQ

How do I control the jump in Chubby Duck Jump?

Hold the mouse button or tap the SPACE key to charge the jump meter, then release to leap. The longer you hold, the farther the duck jumps.

What happens if I charge too little or too long?

Charging too little makes the duck fall short and splash into the meadow below. Holding too long causes it to overshoot the plank and fall into thin air, ending the run.

Is there a time limit in Chubby Duck Jump?

Yes, each run lasts 60 seconds. Your goal is to score as many points as possible by landing on planks before time runs out.
