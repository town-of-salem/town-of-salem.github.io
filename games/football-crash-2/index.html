About Football Crash 2

Football Crash 2 is a fast, free American football runner you can play online without downloading. Grab the ball, sprint down the field, and dodge waves of defenders to score touchdowns and beat your high score. The game challenges you to swerve through the defensive line and survive as long as possible.

Master the Perfect Dodge

In Football Crash 2, you control a ball carrier sprinting downfield while defenders close in from all sides. Your main actions are moving left or right to avoid tackles and collecting coins scattered along the field. Passing close to a tackler triggers a PERFECT DODGE, and chaining these together builds a combo multiplier up to x5, rewarding risk-taking with a higher score.

As you progress, you'll encounter lightning bolts that activate CRASH MODE, letting you smash through everyone in your path, and shields that protect you from one tackle. The game features 24 unlockable teams, each with a unique perk like magnet, kevlar, fast feet, long crash, or double touchdowns. Choosing the right team and timing your dodges are key to setting new personal records.

PLAY BETTER

Football Crash 2 tips

  1. Aim for PERFECT DODGES by passing close to defenders to build a x5 combo for a higher score.
  2. Collect lightning bolts to trigger CRASH MODE and plow through defenders without fear.
  3. Grab a shield when you see one; it saves you from one tackle and keeps your run alive.
  4. Unlock different teams to get perks like magnet or fast feet, and choose one that fits your playstyle.
  5. Practice swerving in tight spaces; the closer you cut it, the more points you earn.
QUICK ANSWERS

Football Crash 2 FAQ

How do you get a PERFECT DODGE in Football Crash 2?

Pass very close to a defender without getting tackled. Chaining PERFECT DODGES in a row increases your combo multiplier up to x5, boosting your score.

What does CRASH MODE do in Football Crash 2?

CRASH MODE is triggered by collecting a lightning bolt. While active, you smash through defenders without being stopped, allowing you to advance safely and score more easily.

How many teams are there in Football Crash 2?

There are 24 teams to unlock, each with its own unique perk such as magnet, kevlar, fast feet, long crash, or double touchdowns.
