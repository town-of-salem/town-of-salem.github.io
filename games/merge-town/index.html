About Merge Town!

Merge Town! puts you in the mayor's seat, ready to turn a simple plot of land into a bustling metropolis. The core objective is straightforward: merge matching houses to create larger homes, which in turn boost your earnings and allow you to purchase even more houses.

Merging for Growth

In Merge Town!, your primary action is tapping on houses to merge them. When you combine two identical houses, they form a bigger home, which increases the income you generate. This simple yet addictive loop keeps you engaged as you watch your town expand and your earnings climb.

As your income grows, you can buy more houses and accelerate the clock to speed up development. The challenge lies in managing your resources efficiently and deciding when to merge versus when to save for new purchases. With each merge, your town becomes more prosperous, offering a satisfying sense of progression.

PLAY BETTER

Merge Town! tips

  1. Always merge houses of the same type to maximize your income growth.
  2. Save your earnings to buy more houses, as each new house adds to your overall production.
  3. Use the clock boost strategically when you have enough houses to make the most of the time skip.
  4. Focus on merging higher-tier houses first, as they provide a larger income boost.
QUICK ANSWERS

Merge Town! FAQ

How do I merge houses in Merge Town!?

Simply tap on one house, then tap on another house of the same type. They will merge into a larger home, which increases your earnings.

What is the main objective in Merge Town!?

Your goal is to turn a plot of land into a thriving town by merging houses, earning money, and buying more houses to expand.

How can I speed up development in Merge Town!?

You can tap the clock icon to fast-forward time, which accelerates the earning process and helps you develop your town faster.
