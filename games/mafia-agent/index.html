About Mafia Agent

Mafia Agent puts you in the role of a professional hitman operating within a city controlled by rival Mafia syndicates. Your objective is to complete a series of contracted kills, each with its own target and circumstances. As you move through the city, you'll need to use your shooting skills to eliminate targets and advance your reputation.

Executing Contracted Kills

The core gameplay loop in Mafia Agent revolves around accepting contracts and then tracking down your targets. Each mission presents a different kill, requiring you to navigate the city and engage in shooting encounters. Your success depends on your aim and your ability to react quickly, as targets may be guarded or attempt to escape.

As you progress, the contracts become more varied, testing your adaptability and precision. You'll face different syndicates, each with its own challenges. The game emphasizes careful aiming and decisive action, rewarding players who can stay calm under pressure and make every shot count. Your performance on each job determines your standing in the criminal underworld.

PLAY BETTER

Mafia Agent tips

  1. Take your time to line up accurate shots; a well-placed hit is more effective than rapid firing.
  2. Watch for moving targets and lead your shots slightly to compensate for their movement.
  3. Prioritize targets that pose an immediate threat, such as armed guards.
  4. Use the environment to your advantage by positioning yourself for clear sightlines.

Mafia Agent FAQ

What is the main objective in Mafia Agent?

Your objective is to complete a series of contracted kills against targets from rival Mafia syndicates within the city.

How do I control my character in Mafia Agent?

You control your aim with the mouse and shoot by left-clicking. There are no other controls needed.

Are there different types of contracts in Mafia Agent?

Yes, the game features a range of different contracted kills, each with its own target and circumstances.
