About Speen

Speen is a multiplayer game built around a single, elegant idea: swing one circle around another and switch which one spins with a tap. Your momentum is your only weapon, so you'll use it to collect glowing pickups, smash through spike balls, and take down a boss bumper to unlock home decoration.

Mastering the Swing

The core loop is simple: tap to swap which circle is the pivot and which swings around it. Swing over green pickups with your orbiting circle to fill one meter, and plant a leg on pink pickups with your standing circle to fill the other. Once both meters are full, you're ready to charge up and smash through obstacles and rivals.

Challenge comes from timing and positioning. Spike balls and rival spinners require a charged leg to break through, and the boss bumper demands careful momentum management. After that, you enter a shared multiplayer world where you paint territory, duel other players, and chase achievements. It's easy to learn but offers a fresh feel in the genre.

PLAY BETTER

Speen tips

  1. Time your swaps to maintain momentum and avoid losing speed.
  2. Prioritize green pickups with your orbiting circle to fill that meter quickly.
  3. Use your standing circle to collect pink pickups, but watch for threats.
  4. Charge your leg before attempting to smash through spike balls or rival spinners.
  5. In multiplayer, paint territory strategically to control the map.
QUICK ANSWERS

Speen FAQ

How do you switch which circle is spinning in Speen?

Tap or click to swap which circle is the planted pivot and which one swings around it. That's the entire control scheme.

What are the green and pink pickups for?

Green pickups fill a meter when collected with your orbiting circle, while pink pickups fill a meter when collected with your standing circle. Filling both meters allows you to charge your leg for smashing.

What happens after you defeat the boss bumper?

Defeating the boss bumper unlocks home decoration, and you can then enter the shared multiplayer world to paint territory, duel other players, and chase achievements.
