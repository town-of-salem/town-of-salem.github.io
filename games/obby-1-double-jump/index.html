About Obby +1 Double Jump

Obby +1 Double Jump drops you into a high-speed obstacle course where you jump, dash, and race against other players. Your goal is simple: conquer 10+ stages, earn Wins, and unlock epic custom trails and skins, including the legendary Tung-Tung-Tung Sahur.

Master the Double Jump

The core loop is straightforward: navigate floating platforms, avoid falls, and reach the end of each stage to earn Wins. Every jump and click grants XP, and leveling up unlocks the coveted Double Jump, which lets you reach higher platforms and take shortcuts. The race is on as you compete against others in real time.

As you progress, stages become more challenging, testing your timing and precision. The Double Jump is a game-changer, but mastering it requires practice. With 10+ stages to conquer, you'll need to balance speed and accuracy to stay ahead. Unlockable trails and skins, including the legendary Tung-Tung-Tung Sahur, add a layer of personalization and bragging rights.

PLAY BETTER

Obby +1 Double Jump tips

  1. Use the Double Jump to skip tricky sections and overtake rivals.
  2. Click frequently to gain XP faster and unlock the Double Jump sooner.
  3. Memorize stage layouts to plan efficient routes.
  4. Watch other players to discover shortcuts and safe landing spots.
  5. Time your jumps carefully to avoid falling off floating platforms.
QUICK ANSWERS

Obby +1 Double Jump FAQ

How do I unlock the Double Jump in Obby +1 Double Jump?

You unlock the Double Jump by leveling up. Gain XP by clicking or jumping, and once you reach the required level, the Double Jump becomes available.

What is the goal in Obby +1 Double Jump?

The goal is to navigate through 10+ stages of floating platforms, reach the end of each stage to earn Wins, and unlock custom trails and skins.

What controls do I use in Obby +1 Double Jump?

Use WASD to walk, Space to jump, and hold the Right Mouse Button to look around. Clicking also grants XP.
