Blacktop Police Chase

Blacktop Police Chase is a low-poly 3D getaway driving game where you pick up a bank robber, evade escalating police pursuit, and reach the hideout.

About Blacktop Police Chase

Blacktop Police Chase puts you behind the wheel of a getaway car in a sprawling low-poly 3D city. Your job: pick up the bank robber, shake off the pursuing police, and deliver him to the hideout. A green arrow guides your route, but the chase is never simple.

Escalating Police Pursuit

The core loop is straightforward: drive to the pickup, then race to the hideout while dodging traffic and cops. The police chase escalates through six levels of pursuit, meaning the longer you stay out, the more aggressive they become. Every crash damages your car, so you must balance speed with caution.

Coins are scattered along the route, and you can spend them in the garage to unlock one of 12 cars, each with distinct speed, grip, weight, and health stats. You can also choose from 12 paint colors. If you're short on coins, you can watch an ad to get a car or repair for free.

PLAY BETTER

Blacktop Police Chase tips

  1. Follow the green arrow but plan your route to avoid heavy traffic and tight alleys.
  2. Brake before corners to reduce crash damage and maintain control.
  3. Prioritize reaching the hideout over collecting coins if your car is badly damaged.
  4. Unlock cars with higher health if you tend to crash often.
  5. Use the look back (B) to check police positions and time your evasive moves.
QUICK ANSWERS

Blacktop Police Chase FAQ

How does the police pursuit work in Blacktop Police Chase?

The police chase escalates through six levels of pursuit. The longer you stay out, the more aggressive the police become, with more units and faster responses. Your car takes damage from crashes, so you need to reach the hideout before it falls apart.

What can I do with coins in the garage?

Coins collected during the chase can be spent in the garage to unlock 12 different cars, each with its own speed, grip, weight, and health stats. You can also choose from 12 paint colors. Alternatively, you can watch an ad to get a car or a repair for free.

What are the controls for Blacktop Police Chase on PC and mobile?

On PC, use Arrow Keys, WASD, or ZQSD to drive, Spacebar/S/Arrow Down to brake, B to look back, and Right Shift to reset. On mobile, the game uses on-screen touch controls.
