About Drunk Basket

Drunk Basket is a hilarious 1-2 player active-ragdoll basketball game. You control wobbly, physics-driven players and time your shots to sink baskets before your rival. Play solo against the AI or go head-to-head with a friend on the same device, with every round throwing in a random twist.

Master the Wobbly Shot

The core loop in Drunk Basket revolves around grabbing the ball and charging your shot. Hold the designated key to build power, then release to throw. Because your player is a ragdoll, movement and aim are deliberately unstable, so you must adjust your timing and positioning to get the ball into the hoop.

Each round introduces a random twist, such as low gravity or a giant ball, which changes how you approach the shot. These modifiers keep matches unpredictable, forcing you to adapt quickly. Whether you're facing the AI or a friend, the challenge lies in controlling your clumsy character and making the most of each unique condition.

PLAY BETTER

Drunk Basket tips

  1. Get close to the ball to grab it quickly before your opponent does.
  2. Charge your shot fully for more power, but release inside the green zone for accuracy.
  3. Watch for the random twist each round, like low gravity, and adjust your throw strength accordingly.
  4. If playing against a friend, try to disrupt their positioning by moving your ragdoll into their path.
QUICK ANSWERS

Drunk Basket FAQ

How do you play Drunk Basket?

You control a wobbly ragdoll basketball player. Grab the ball, hold the charge button to set your shot power, and release to throw. The goal is to sink baskets before your opponent, whether you're playing solo against the AI or with a friend on the same device.

What are the controls in Drunk Basket?

Player 1 uses the W key to charge and release shots. Player 2 uses the Up Arrow key. On mobile, you use on-screen buttons instead of the keyboard.

What makes Drunk Basket different each round?

Every round includes a random twist, such as low gravity or a giant ball. These modifiers change the physics and challenge, making each match unpredictable and requiring you to adapt your strategy.
