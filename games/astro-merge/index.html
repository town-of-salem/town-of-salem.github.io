About Astro Merge

Astro Merge is a fast-paced puzzle game that challenges you to drop planets onto a growing stack. Match identical planets to merge them into larger ones, score points, and keep the pressure from overwhelming you.

Master the Merge Chain

In Astro Merge, you control where each planet falls by moving your mouse or finger to aim. Click or tap to release the planet onto the stack. When two planets of the same size touch, they merge into a bigger one, clearing space and adding to your score. The key is to plan your drops so that matches happen naturally as the stack grows.

The challenge ramps up as the stack gets taller and the pressure to act quickly increases. You must think fast to avoid letting the stack reach the top. Each successful merge gives you breathing room, but the risk of a misdrop grows. Your goal is to keep merging and beat your own high score.

PLAY BETTER

Astro Merge tips

  1. Aim for gaps between planets to create side-by-side matches.
  2. Drop larger planets first to clear space for smaller ones.
  3. Plan two moves ahead to set up chain merges.
  4. When the stack gets high, focus on quick, accurate drops to avoid overflow.

Astro Merge FAQ

How do you play Astro Merge?

You aim with your mouse or finger and click or tap to drop planets onto a stack. Matching planets merge to grow your stack and score points.

What happens when planets match in Astro Merge?

When two planets of the same size touch, they merge into a larger planet, which clears space and increases your score.

What is the main objective in Astro Merge?

The main objective is to keep merging planets to build the biggest stack possible while avoiding letting the stack reach the top, all while aiming for a high score.
