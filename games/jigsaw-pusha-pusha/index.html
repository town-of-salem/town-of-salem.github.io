About Jigsaw: Pusha Pusha

Jigsaw: Pusha Pusha is a puzzle game set in a forest where your goal is to push crates onto altars. Using the arrow keys or W A S D, you walk and push each crate into place. Successfully placing all crates pleases the almighty God and completes the level.

Mastering the Push

The core loop in Jigsaw: Pusha Pusha is straightforward: move your character, push a crate, and navigate it to the altar. Each level presents a new arrangement of crates and altars, requiring you to plan your moves carefully. You can only push, not pull, so think ahead to avoid getting stuck.

The forest setting adds a calm atmosphere, but the puzzles grow trickier as you progress. You'll need to consider the order in which you push crates and the paths you take. With patience and logical thinking, you can solve each puzzle and satisfy the almighty God.

PLAY BETTER

Jigsaw: Pusha Pusha tips

  1. Plan your moves before pushing to avoid getting stuck.
  2. Push crates along the edges of the altar to align them easily.
  3. Use the environment to your advantage, like walls to guide crates.
  4. If a crate is stuck, restart the level to try a different approach.

Jigsaw: Pusha Pusha FAQ

How do I control the character in Jigsaw: Pusha Pusha?

You can use the W A S D keys or the arrow keys to move your character. Simply walk into a crate to push it.

What is the objective of Jigsaw: Pusha Pusha?

The goal is to push each crate onto an altar. Once all crates are placed, you please the almighty God and complete the level.

Can I pull crates in Jigsaw: Pusha Pusha?

No, you can only push crates. This means you need to plan your moves carefully to avoid pushing a crate into an unreachable spot.
