About MINE BLADE Online KitPvP

MINE BLADE Online KitPvP drops you into a 3D arena where the goal is simple: defeat other players in sword combat and prove you're the strongest. Every victory earns you money, which you can spend on new costumes to customize your fighter. It's a quick, competitive loop of fighting, earning, and styling.

Earn and Customize

The core loop is straightforward: enter the arena, equip your sword, and engage in fast-paced PvP battles. Move quickly, time your attacks, and land hits to defeat opponents. Each win adds money to your balance, directly rewarding your combat skill.

Your earnings open up a customization layer. Spend your hard-won money on different costumes to change your character's look. This creates a tangible goal beyond each match—build your style while climbing the ranks. The challenge is balancing aggressive play to earn more with the need to stay alive and outmaneuver skilled rivals.

PLAY BETTER

MINE BLADE Online KitPvP tips

  1. Time your attacks to land hits when opponents are close, not just swinging wildly.
  2. Use sprint (W W) to close gaps quickly or retreat when low on health.
  3. Jump to avoid sword swings and create openings for counterattacks.
  4. Save your earnings to buy costumes that suit your playstyle, as they also show your progress.

MINE BLADE Online KitPvP FAQ

What is the main goal in MINE BLADE Online KitPvP?

The main goal is to defeat other players in PvP sword battles to earn money. You can then spend that money on costumes to customize your character.

How do I control my character in MINE BLADE Online KitPvP?

On PC, use WASD to move, Space to jump, and Left Mouse Button to attack. Press W twice to sprint. On mobile, use the touch controls provided.

Can I play MINE BLADE Online KitPvP on mobile?

Yes, the game is playable on PC and mobile, with touch controls available for mobile devices.
