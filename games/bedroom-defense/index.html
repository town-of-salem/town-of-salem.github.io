About Bedroom Defense

Bedroom Defense puts you in a terrifying nightly struggle to keep your bedroom safe from waves of monsters. As a horror survival strategy game, your main goal is to reinforce doors, set up defenses, and upgrade your bed to make it through each night until dawn.

Reinforce and Upgrade Your Defenses

Your core loop in Bedroom Defense is a tense cycle of preparation and survival. Each night, monsters attack your room, and you must use your mouse to manage defenses, equip weapons, and reinforce doors. Between waves, you'll upgrade your bed and other equipment to increase your chances of holding out longer.

As nights progress, enemies become stronger and more dangerous, forcing you to make strategic decisions about resource management. You'll need to balance spending on immediate repairs versus long-term upgrades, all while the horror atmosphere ramps up. The challenge is to survive until morning, but every night tests your planning and adaptability.

PLAY BETTER

Bedroom Defense tips

  1. Reinforce doors early to buy time before enemies break through.
  2. Upgrade your bed regularly, as it likely provides health or other survival benefits.
  3. Balance spending between immediate repairs and long-term upgrades to avoid running out of resources.
  4. Equip powerful weapons before the night starts to be ready for stronger waves.
  5. Manage resources wisely; don't overspend on one area and leave yourself vulnerable elsewhere.
QUICK ANSWERS

Bedroom Defense FAQ

What is the main objective in Bedroom Defense?

Your main objective is to survive each night by protecting your bedroom from waves of monsters. You do this by reinforcing doors, building defenses, equipping weapons, and upgrading your bed to last until morning.

How do I control the game in Bedroom Defense?

The game is played entirely with the mouse. You use mouse clicks to interact with objects, build defenses, and equip weapons, and you move the mouse to aim and target enemies.

What happens as the game progresses in Bedroom Defense?

As you survive more nights, the monsters become stronger and more dangerous. This means you'll need to continually upgrade your defenses and manage resources carefully to keep up with the increasing threat.
