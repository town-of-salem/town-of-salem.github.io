About Bee Colony

In Bee Colony, you take charge of a hive under attack. This adventure game blends strategy and skill as you drag items to arrange your defenses. Your goal is to protect your territory against relentless waves of enemies, making every move count.

Strategic Item Placement

The core gameplay revolves around dragging items with your mouse to position them strategically. You'll manage weapons, armor, and supplies, but your backpack space is limited. Each item you place affects your survival, so think carefully about what to equip and where to put it.

As you progress through chapters, the challenges evolve, and foes become more powerful. Your decisions on item management and placement are crucial. With an idle element, you can also plan for gradual upgrades. Prove your skills as the ultimate defender against the invasion.

PLAY BETTER

Bee Colony tips

  1. Prioritize placing weapons early to fend off the first waves.
  2. Balance your backpack with both offensive and defensive items.
  3. Upgrade your gear when possible to handle stronger foes.
  4. Plan your item layout to cover all attack angles.

Bee Colony FAQ

What is the main objective in Bee Colony?

Your goal is to defend your territory by strategically placing items to survive waves of monster attacks.

How do I control the game?

You control the game by dragging items with the left mouse button to position them on the battlefield.

Is there a progression system?

Yes, you progress through chapters, each presenting evolving challenges and more powerful enemies.
