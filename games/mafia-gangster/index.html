About Mafia Gangster

Mafia Gangster puts you in the middle of a gang war. Your goal is to defend your territory from waves of bandits trying to invade. With 40 levels, 12 enemy types, and 6 upgradeable weapons, you'll need to aim carefully and manage your resources to survive.

Aim, Shoot, and Upgrade

In Mafia Gangster, you touch the war zone on screen to aim your weapon at incoming bandits. Each mission rewards you with points for successful defenses, which you can spend to strengthen your arsenal and fortify your field. The core loop is simple: spot the threat, shoot it down, and use your earnings to prepare for the next assault.

As you progress through 40 levels, you'll face 12 different enemy types, each with its own behavior and toughness. Choosing which of the 6 weapons to upgrade is a key strategic decision. Do you invest in firepower or rate of fire? Your choices determine how well you hold the line against increasingly aggressive waves.

PLAY BETTER

Mafia Gangster tips

  1. Prioritize upgrading a single weapon type early to maximize its effectiveness.
  2. Aim for headshots or weak points if enemies have them, to take them down faster.
  3. Watch for enemy patterns and adjust your aim accordingly.
  4. Save points for upgrades that suit your playstyle, whether that's speed or power.
  5. Don't neglect field upgrades—they can provide crucial defensive bonuses.
QUICK ANSWERS

Mafia Gangster FAQ

How do I control the game?

You control the game by touching the war zone on your screen to aim at targets. The weapon fires automatically when aimed at an enemy.

What is the main objective in Mafia Gangster?

Your main objective is to defend your territory from bandits trying to invade. You must survive all 40 levels by shooting enemies and upgrading your weapons and field.

How many weapons and enemies are there?

There are 6 different upgradeable weapons and 12 different enemy types. You can strengthen your weapons and field with points earned in each mission.
