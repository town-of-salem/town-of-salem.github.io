About Race Through Traffic

Race Through Traffic drops you behind the wheel of a supercar on a busy road. Your goal is simple: keep driving, dodge the cars ahead, and survive as long as you can. The farther you travel and the riskier your overtakes, the higher your final score climbs.

Master Risky Overtakes

Each run starts with you accelerating into a stream of traffic. You steer left and right to slip between slower cars, timing your moves to avoid a crash. Every successful pass pushes your score higher, but cutting it close boosts your reward even more. Coins appear along the road, so grabbing them while weaving adds another layer of challenge.

The tension builds as traffic gets denser and your speed increases. You can choose a mode that fits your mood, then focus on the road ahead. Crashes end the run, but each attempt lets you collect coins and unlock new cars. Upgrading speed, handling, and other stats helps you push further and set new personal records.

PLAY BETTER

Race Through Traffic tips

  1. Time your lane changes carefully—wait for a gap before swerving.
  2. Riskier overtakes between cars give a bigger score boost, so judge when it's worth it.
  3. Collect coins even if it means a slight detour, as they help unlock new cars.
  4. Upgrade handling first to make steering more responsive in tight spots.
  5. Stay calm in dense traffic; panicked moves often lead to crashes.
QUICK ANSWERS

Race Through Traffic FAQ

What is the main objective in Race Through Traffic?

The main objective is to drive as far as possible without crashing, overtaking cars to survive longer and earn a higher score.

How do I control my car in Race Through Traffic?

You can steer left with A or the left arrow, right with D or the right arrow, and use the up and down arrows to accelerate or brake when available. On-screen buttons also work.

What happens when I crash in Race Through Traffic?

A crash ends your current run. You'll then see your final score and can start a new attempt, keeping any coins you collected to unlock new cars or upgrade stats.
