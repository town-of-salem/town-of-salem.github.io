About ANT COLONY

ANT COLONY is a relaxing puzzle game where you guide a tiny ant crew to dismantle pixel pictures, block by block. Your goal is to clear the entire image before your five slots fill up with mismatched colors. It's a simple, one-tap challenge that grows more intricate as the pictures expand.

Mastering the Color Queue

In ANT COLONY, each level presents a hand-made pixel picture, from a small apple to a 36x44 mural. You tap a colored box from the queue to send it to a free slot. Ants then march out, pick up bricks of that color, and carry them down the hole, gradually clearing the image. The key is to manage your slots wisely, as they can only hold one color at a time.

As the pictures grow, the color-matching logic tightens, demanding more foresight. With three power-ups—Speed x3, Pick Any, and Shuffle—you can overcome tight spots. You can also unlock extra slots when things get crowded. The game starts simple but becomes tricky, testing your planning and patience. How far can you dig?

PLAY BETTER

ANT COLONY tips

  1. Plan ahead: check which colors are most abundant in the picture and prioritize them.
  2. Keep at least one slot free to avoid getting stuck with no matching color.
  3. Use Shuffle when the queue is unfavorable, but save it for critical moments.
  4. Unlock extra slots early if you find yourself frequently running out of space.
  5. Speed x3 is handy for clearing large areas quickly, but use it when you have a clear plan.
QUICK ANSWERS

ANT COLONY FAQ

How do I play ANT COLONY?

Tap a colored box from the queue to assign it to a free slot. Ants will then carry bricks of that color away from the picture. Clear the entire picture before all five slots are filled with colors that no longer exist in the picture.

What happens if all slots are filled?

If all five slots are occupied and none of the colors in the slots appear in the remaining picture, the level ends. You must manage your slots to avoid this.

What power-ups are available in ANT COLONY?

There are three power-ups: Speed x3, which makes ants work faster; Pick Any, which lets you choose any color from the queue; and Shuffle, which rearranges the queue. You can also unlock extra slots to give yourself more room.
