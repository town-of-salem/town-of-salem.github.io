About GapShot

GapShot is a casual soccer clicker that puts your aim to the test. Your goal is simple: make the soccer ball pass between the legs of the goalkeeper. With limited failures allowed, every shot counts as you try to score without hitting the keeper or sending the ball out of bounds.

Master the Precision Shot

In GapShot, the core loop is straightforward: line up your shot and click to send the ball toward the goalkeeper. The key is to thread the ball through the narrow gap between the keeper's legs. Each successful shot earns you points, but you must stay focused—every miss brings you closer to failure.

The challenge ramps up as you face limited failures. You must decide when to take risks and when to play it safe. Hitting the goalkeeper's legs or sending the ball outside the target area counts as a failure, so precision is everything. With its simple clicker mechanics, GapShot offers a quick, satisfying test of accuracy and nerve.

PLAY BETTER

GapShot tips

  1. Aim for the center of the gap between the legs for the highest chance of success.
  2. Observe the goalkeeper's stance and adjust your aim accordingly.
  3. Don't rush your shots; take a moment to line up the perfect angle.
  4. Stay calm under pressure—panicking can lead to careless misses.

GapShot FAQ

What is the objective of GapShot?

The objective is to make the soccer ball pass between the goalkeeper's legs. You must avoid hitting the legs or sending the ball out of bounds, as you have limited failures allowed.

How do I control the ball in GapShot?

You control the ball by clicking or tapping to shoot. Aim by positioning your cursor or finger to guide the ball's trajectory toward the gap between the goalkeeper's legs.

What happens if I fail in GapShot?

If the ball hits the goalkeeper's legs or goes outside the target area, it counts as a failure. You have a limited number of failures, so too many mistakes will end the game.
