About Real Cars Epic Stunts

Real Cars Epic Stunts delivers an adrenaline-fueled driving challenge where you pilot powerful supercars through stunt-filled tracks. Your goal is to master huge ramps, perform spectacular stunts, and race against the clock while collecting coins and diamonds to unlock new vehicles and upgrades.

Mastering Ramps and Stunts

In Real Cars Epic Stunts, each course is a playground of ramps, loops, and obstacles that test your timing and control. You'll need to accelerate at the right moment to launch off ramps and land cleanly to keep your run going. As you progress, you'll face increasingly complex stunt courses that demand precision and quick reflexes.

Success depends on your ability to balance speed with control. Collecting coins and diamonds scattered across the tracks lets you unlock new supercars and improve their performance. With realistic physics and detailed vehicles, every jump feels weighty, and a single misjudged landing can cost you precious seconds. The game also offers a two-player mode for head-to-head stunt battles.

PLAY BETTER

Real Cars Epic Stunts tips

  1. Build up speed before hitting a ramp to ensure you clear the gap and land on the track.
  2. Aim for the center of the landing zone to avoid flipping over.
  3. Prioritize collecting diamonds over coins when you have to choose, as they may unlock rarer cars.
  4. In two-player mode, watch your opponent's run to learn the best lines and improve your own.
  5. Upgrade your car's handling and acceleration first to make stunts easier to control.
QUICK ANSWERS

Real Cars Epic Stunts FAQ

How do I unlock new cars in Real Cars Epic Stunts?

You unlock new cars by collecting coins and diamonds during stunt runs. These currencies let you purchase and upgrade vehicles from the garage.

Can I play Real Cars Epic Stunts with a friend?

Yes, the game includes a two-player mode where you can compete against another player on the same device, taking turns or racing simultaneously.

What is the main objective in Real Cars Epic Stunts?

The main objective is to complete stunt courses by performing spectacular jumps and stunts while racing against the clock. Collecting coins and diamonds along the way helps you progress.
