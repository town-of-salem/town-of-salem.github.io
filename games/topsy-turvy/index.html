About Topsy Turvy

Topsy Turvy is a spooky-cute arcade puzzler that blends bubble shooter mechanics with a creeping sense of dread. You control a quirky steampunk cannon, firing orbs at a winding track to clear it before a creepy snake slithers into the drain. Match three or more to survive.

Matching Orbs to Stop the Snake

Your cannon sits at the center of the action, and each click or tap sends an orb toward the spot you choose. The track fills with eyeballs and skull orbs that inch forward like a living serpent. Aim carefully to land your shot next to matching colors, forming groups of three or more to pop them and clear space.

As the snake crawls closer, every decision matters. Chain combos by clearing clusters that cause nearby orbs to fall, buying precious time. The challenge ramps up as the track tightens and the pace quickens. Simple to pick up, but tricky to master, Topsy Turvy keeps you on edge with its playful horror theme.

PLAY BETTER

Topsy Turvy tips

  1. Aim for clusters that are near the front of the snake to buy the most time.
  2. Look for opportunities to drop orbs that cause a chain reaction, clearing multiple groups at once.
  3. When the track is crowded, focus on breaking up long lines of the same color to prevent the snake from advancing.
  4. Use the cannon's position to your advantage by targeting orbs that are just behind the leading orb to create matches.
  5. Stay calm and plan your shots, as a single well-placed orb can turn the tide.
QUICK ANSWERS

Topsy Turvy FAQ

What is the goal in Topsy Turvy?

Your goal is to clear the track of creepy eyeballs and skull orbs before the snake reaches the drain. Match three or more orbs to remove them and keep the snake from advancing.

How do I control the cannon in Topsy Turvy?

You control the cannon by clicking or tapping wherever you want to send the orb. The cannon will fire toward that point, so aim carefully to hit the orbs you want to match.

What happens if the snake reaches the drain?

If the snake reaches the drain, the game ends. To prevent this, keep clearing orbs and chaining combos to slow the snake's progress and survive longer.
