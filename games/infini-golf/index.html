About Infini Golf

Infini Golf is an arcade sports game that turns golf into a nonstop challenge. Instead of waiting for a full round, you move from one hole to the next instantly. Your goal is simple: aim carefully, choose your shot power, and get the ball into the hole to keep the streak going.

Master Your Shot Power

The core loop in Infini Golf is quick and engaging. Each hole presents a new layout, and you must set the direction and force of your shot with precision. A gentle tap or a powerful swing can make all the difference, so reading the terrain and adjusting your power is key to sinking the ball efficiently.

As you play, you'll occasionally collect dollars after successful shots. These dollars are your currency for unlocking different ball designs, like the coral, green, or yellow options. This progression adds a light layer of customization, giving you a reason to keep playing and experiment with different looks as you tackle the endless course.

PLAY BETTER

Infini Golf tips

  1. Pay attention to the terrain slope and obstacles to judge the right power level.
  2. Start with a lighter touch on short holes to avoid overshooting the cup.
  3. Collect dollars whenever you can, as they are the only way to unlock new ball designs.
  4. Practice your aim on early holes to build a feel for the game's physics.
QUICK ANSWERS

Infini Golf FAQ

How does the endless format work in Infini Golf?

In Infini Golf, you don't have to wait between holes. As soon as you sink one ball, the next hole appears immediately, so you can keep playing without breaks.

What are the dollars used for in Infini Golf?

Dollars are collected occasionally when you hit shots. You use them to unlock different balls, such as the coral, green, and yellow ones, giving you visual variety.

What is the main objective in Infini Golf?

The main objective is to get the ball into the hole on each course. You must set the direction and power of your shot carefully to succeed and continue to the next hole.
