About Upgrade the CARS

Upgrade the CARS is a 3D driving game that turns every gate into a decision. Steer through green plus gates to boost your engine, body, and speed, while dodging red minus gates that strip your upgrades. Reach the end of the track with the strongest car you can build, then cash it in to fund your next run.

Gate Strategy and Risk

The core loop is simple: drive, hit the right gates, and avoid the wrong ones. Each green gate adds a boost to your car's performance, while red gates take away what you've earned. The challenge is balancing speed with caution—sometimes the safest path isn't the fastest, and a risky line through multiple plus gates can pay off big if you pull it off.

As you progress, the road gets tougher. Giant kaiju block your path, and you'll need weapons to clear them. Money collected on every lap lets you buy new parking spots, unlock fresh cars in the garage, and repaint them in the texture shop. Every run funds the next, creating a satisfying cycle of improvement and customization.

PLAY BETTER

Upgrade the CARS tips

  1. Prioritize green gates early to build a strong car before the track gets crowded.
  2. Watch for red gates and steer around them—losing upgrades can set you back significantly.
  3. Save money to unlock new parking spots, which may lead to better cars or upgrades.
  4. When kaiju appear, focus on collecting weapons to clear them and keep your run going.
QUICK ANSWERS

Upgrade the CARS FAQ

How do I upgrade my car in Upgrade the CARS?

Drive through green plus gates on the track to boost your engine, body, and speed. Avoid red minus gates that remove upgrades.

What can I do with the money I collect?

Use earnings to buy new parking spots, unlock fresh cars in the garage, and repaint them in the texture shop.

What happens when I reach the end of the track?

You cash in your car for money, which you can spend on upgrades and new vehicles. Further runs may encounter giant kaiju that require weapons to clear.
