About Fairy Town

Fairy Town is a bubble shooter puzzle game that combines classic match-3 mechanics with a sweet candy-saving twist. Your goal is to aim, match, and pop bubbles of the same color to clear each level and rescue the candies trapped inside bubble traps.

Matching Bubbles to Save Candies

In Fairy Town, you tap to shoot a bubble toward the cluster above. When three or more bubbles of the same color connect, they pop and disappear, bringing you closer to clearing the level. Some levels hide candies inside bubble traps, so you must strategically pop surrounding bubbles to free them.

As you progress, you'll earn stars and gems based on your performance, which you can spend on boosters to help with tricky layouts. Daily bonuses and new levels arrive every two weeks, keeping the challenge fresh. The game is simple to pick up but offers enough variety to keep you engaged.

PLAY BETTER

Fairy Town tips

  1. Aim for clusters of three or more same-colored bubbles to clear them efficiently.
  2. Prioritize freeing trapped candies early to avoid difficult positions later.
  3. Use boosters strategically when you're close to completing a level.
  4. Collect daily bonuses to stock up on gems and boosters.
  5. Plan your shots to create chain reactions and clear more bubbles at once.

Fairy Town FAQ

How do I play Fairy Town?

Tap to shoot bubbles toward the cluster. Match three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them and clear the level. Some levels require saving candies trapped in bubbles.

What are the controls in Fairy Town?

The game uses simple tap controls. Tap on the screen to aim and shoot a bubble toward the cluster.

What is the objective in Fairy Town?

The main objective is to clear all bubbles from the board by matching three or more of the same color. In some levels, you also need to rescue candies trapped inside bubbles.
