About Mononinja

Mononinja is a 1-player arcade adventure that puts your jumping precision to the test. You guide a pixel ninja through over 30 levels, each packed with deadly obstacles. Your goal is simple: reach the end without succumbing to certain death. Are you ready to prove you have what it takes to be a shinobi?

Mastering the Jump

The core of Mononinja revolves around perfectly timed jumps. You control the height of each leap by how long you hold the jump button. Short taps clear small gaps, while longer holds send your ninja soaring over larger hazards. Every level demands a careful read of the terrain ahead.

With over 30 levels, the challenge escalates as you progress. Obstacles become more frequent and require quicker decisions. The game tests your patience and reflexes, rewarding precise timing over reckless speed. Each successful run brings you closer to mastering the way of the ninja.

PLAY BETTER

Mononinja tips

  1. Hold the jump button longer for higher jumps over tall obstacles.
  2. Release early for short, precise hops over small gaps.
  3. Study the level layout before jumping to plan your route.
  4. Stay patient—rushing often leads to mistimed jumps and certain death.
QUICK ANSWERS

Mononinja FAQ

How do I control the jump height in Mononinja?

The longer you press and hold the jump button (SPACE on desktop, screen on mobile), the higher your ninja jumps. Release quickly for a short hop.

How many levels are in Mononinja?

Mononinja features over 30 levels, each filled with deadly obstacles and increasing challenges.

What happens if I hit an obstacle in Mononinja?

Hitting any obstacle results in certain death, forcing you to restart the level. Precision and careful timing are essential to survive.
