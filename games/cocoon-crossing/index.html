About Cocoon Crossing

Cocoon Crossing is a cozy 3D routing puzzle that asks you to guide each caterpillar to its own colored cocoon. Drag a worm's head or tail, and it crawls tile by tile across a sunlit garden board. The challenge is to untangle paths so every caterpillar reaches its destination.

Mastering Over-and-Under Routes

In Cocoon Crossing, you control caterpillars by dragging their head or tail to make them crawl along the board. The twist is that two caterpillars cannot share a tile on the same level. At a crossing, you must route one over a bridge and the other under a tunnel, creating a satisfying puzzle of layered paths.

As you progress through 140 handcrafted levels, you'll face obstacles like rocks, mud, thorns, ice, and one-way gates. These force you to plan your moves carefully. For a more relaxed experience, try the endless Zen mode or tackle the daily puzzle. Collectible butterflies add an extra layer of completion.

PLAY BETTER

Cocoon Crossing tips

  1. Plan the entire route before moving a caterpillar to avoid blocking yourself.
  2. Use bridges and tunnels to separate overlapping paths, allowing two caterpillars to cross safely.
  3. Watch for one-way gates and plan your approach to pass through them in the correct direction.
  4. In levels with ice, remember that caterpillars slide until they hit an obstacle, so use it to your advantage.
  5. If you get stuck, try moving a different caterpillar first to open up space.
QUICK ANSWERS

Cocoon Crossing FAQ

How do I control the caterpillars in Cocoon Crossing?

You use your mouse or touch to drag a caterpillar's head or tail. The caterpillar will then crawl in that direction, tile by tile, until it reaches an obstacle or the edge of the board.

What is the main objective in Cocoon Crossing?

The goal is to guide each caterpillar to the cocoon of its own color. You must untangle their paths so they don't block each other, using bridges and tunnels to cross over and under.

What kinds of challenges can I expect in Cocoon Crossing?

You'll face obstacles like rocks, mud, thorns, ice, and one-way gates. These add complexity to your routing decisions. The game includes 140 handcrafted levels, an endless Zen mode, and a daily puzzle.
