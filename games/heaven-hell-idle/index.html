About Heaven & Hell Idle

Heaven & Hell Idle puts you in charge of two very different realms at once. This 3D idle arcade management game challenges you to build attractions, welcome visitors, and earn money across heavenly clouds and fiery hell zones. Your goal is to expand both sides efficiently and create a thriving entertainment empire.

Balancing Dual Realms

The core loop revolves around constructing and upgrading facilities in both the heavenly and hellish areas. As visitors arrive, you collect income and reinvest it into new attractions and upgrades. Hiring helpers automates income generation, letting you focus on strategic expansion rather than manual collection.

The real challenge lies in managing two worlds simultaneously. Each realm offers unique attractions and requires distinct upgrades to maximize profitability. You must decide where to allocate resources, balancing development between heaven and hell to ensure steady growth. As your character and businesses improve, your income increases, unlocking further expansion opportunities.

PLAY BETTER

Heaven & Hell Idle tips

  1. Prioritize hiring helpers early to automate income collection, freeing you to focus on expansion.
  2. Balance upgrades between heaven and hell to avoid stagnation in one realm.
  3. Unlock new attractions as soon as possible to boost visitor numbers and income.
  4. Regularly check both areas for available upgrades to maximize efficiency.
QUICK ANSWERS

Heaven & Hell Idle FAQ

What is the main objective in Heaven & Hell Idle?

The main objective is to build and expand two different worlds—heavenly and hellish—by welcoming visitors, earning money, unlocking attractions, and upgrading facilities to create a profitable idle entertainment empire.

How do controls work in Heaven & Hell Idle?

You move your character using WASD or arrow keys, and use the mouse to interact with buttons and menus. This allows you to navigate both realms and manage your businesses.

Is Heaven & Hell Idle an idle game?

Yes, it's an idle management game. You can hire helpers to automate income generation, allowing progress to continue even when you're not actively playing, though you'll still need to make strategic decisions for expansion.
