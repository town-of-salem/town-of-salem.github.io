About Phone Call Simulator: Monsters

Phone Call Simulator: Monsters turns your screen into a fake phone full of surprise calls from characters like Labubu, Huggy Wuggy, and Skibidi Toilet. Answer the ringing phone, chat with funny monsters, and use the keypad to dial numbers. Tap the toilet to earn coins and unlock new photos and wallpapers.

Earn Coins and Customize Your Phone

The core loop is simple: your phone rings, and you choose to answer or hang up. Each call brings a different character, from Officer Labubu to Grandma, with silly conversations and prank messages. Open Chats to reply to texts, and use the Keypad to dial 911 or a friend's number for extra surprises.

Coins are the key to progression. Tap the toilet repeatedly to rack up coins, then spend them on upgrades, new photos, and wallpapers. You can also customize your phone's look. The game is a lighthearted simulator for kids who enjoy playful interactions and collecting visual rewards without any real challenge or pressure.

PLAY BETTER

Phone Call Simulator: Monsters tips

  1. Tap the toilet rapidly to maximize coin earnings.
  2. Answer every call to discover all the characters and their funny dialogues.
  3. Use the keypad to dial 911 or a friend's number to see what happens.
  4. Save your coins to unlock new wallpapers and photos that personalize your phone.

Phone Call Simulator: Monsters FAQ

How do I answer and end calls in Phone Call Simulator: Monsters?

Tap the green button to answer a call and the red button to hang up. You can choose to talk or end the call whenever you like.

What is the purpose of tapping the toilet?

Tapping the toilet earns you coins, which you can spend on upgrades, photos, and wallpapers to customize your phone.

Can I dial numbers on the keypad?

Yes, use the keypad to dial numbers like 911 or a friend's number to trigger special calls and reactions.
