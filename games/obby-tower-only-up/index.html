About Obby Tower: Only Up!

Obby Tower: Only Up! is a 3D parkour obby where the only way is up. Climb a giant sky tower through 30 levels, avoid traps, and reach the top. Start in the Training Hall to learn each obstacle safely, then begin your ascent.

Master the Vertical Obstacle Course

Your climb is a series of jumps and sprints across platforms high above the ground. Springs launch you skyward, hammers and crushers try to knock you off, and conveyors push you in unexpected directions. Wind zones can carry you or throw you off course, while cannons offer a quick boost if timed well.

The tower tests your timing and precision with collapsing platforms and vanishing bridges that disappear underfoot. One wrong jump sends you falling, so plan each move. Collect coins and diamonds to unlock skins like Noob, Cat, Dog, Frog, Capybara, and Spider. If a level stumps you, skip it and continue upward.

PLAY BETTER

Obby Tower: Only Up! tips

  1. Use the Training Hall to learn trap patterns before attempting the real climb.
  2. Sprint before jumping to clear wider gaps and avoid falling.
  3. Watch for wind zones—they can push you off platforms if you're not careful.
  4. Collect coins and diamonds to unlock new skins and add a personal touch.
  5. If a level is too tricky, use the skip option to keep progressing.
QUICK ANSWERS

Obby Tower: Only Up! FAQ

What is the goal in Obby Tower: Only Up!?

The goal is to climb a giant sky tower through 30 levels, avoiding traps and obstacles, to reach the top.

How do I control my character in Obby Tower: Only Up!?

On PC, use WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to look, Space to jump, and Shift to sprint. On mobile, use the left joystick to move, swipe the right side to look, and use on-screen buttons for jump and sprint.

What happens if I fall in Obby Tower: Only Up!?

If you fall, you'll lose progress and need to climb back up. The Training Hall helps you learn traps safely before attempting the full climb.
