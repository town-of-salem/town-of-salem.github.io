About Eco Ninja

Eco Ninja is a casual match-3 puzzle game that blends recycling with a playful ninja theme. The main objective is to chain three or more items of the same type to clear them from the board and increase your score. It's designed to be easy for kids to pick up and enjoy.

Chaining Recyclables for High Scores

In Eco Ninja, the core loop revolves around spotting groups of identical recyclable items and linking them together. When you chain three or more of the same type, they disappear, and your score climbs. The game encourages quick thinking and pattern recognition as you scan the board for the best possible matches.

As you play, the challenge lies in finding larger chains to maximize your score per move. Since the game is tailored for kids, the difficulty stays approachable, but there's still room to improve your efficiency. Each successful chain feels rewarding, and the ninja theme adds a fun, energetic vibe to the puzzle-solving action.

PLAY BETTER

Eco Ninja tips

  1. Look for larger groups of the same item to create longer chains for more points.
  2. Scan the whole board before making a move to spot the best chain available.
  3. Prioritize chains that are easy to reach and quick to complete to keep your momentum going.
  4. Practice recognizing patterns faster to improve your score over time.
QUICK ANSWERS

Eco Ninja FAQ

What is the main objective in Eco Ninja?

The main objective is to chain three or more recyclable items of the same type to clear them and increase your score.

How do you play Eco Ninja?

You select and drag through three or more matching items to form a chain. When you release, the items disappear and you earn points.

Is Eco Ninja suitable for kids?

Yes, Eco Ninja is designed to be very easy for kids to score, with simple mechanics and a fun ninja theme.

Start by looking at the grid filled with recyclable items. Your goal is to find and select three or more items of the same type that are connected in a line. Tap or click on the first item, then drag through the others to form a chain. Release to confirm the match, and the items will disappear, adding points to your score. Keep chaining to rack up bigger combos and watch your score grow.
