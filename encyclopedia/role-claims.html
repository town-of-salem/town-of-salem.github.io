Town of Salem Role Claims: A Practical Verification Framework

A role-by-role checklist of the evidence to request, the limits of that evidence and the exceptions that can make a plausible claim look false.

A role claim sets a limit on what the speaker could know. Use this Town of Salem role claims reference to identify the evidence to request; use the claim guide when you need help presenting your own role.

What each common claim should contain

Mayor The public reveal when available Typing “Mayor” is not the same as revealing.

Distinguish missing detail from impossibility

A nervous Doctor may forget a night number. That is missing detail which can be requested. A claim with no possible role-list slot is a different problem, but only after cleaned or forged deaths and flexible slots have been accounted for. Do not assign the same confidence to both.

Ask without supplying a safe answer

“Who did you visit on Night 2?” preserves the player’s own account. “You visited 7, right?” feeds them the detail you hoped to test. Collect the independent log first, then compare it with the reported visit.

Example: an apparently confirmed visitor

A Lookout names Player 8 at the victim’s house. Player 8 claims a passive role. That discrepancy deserves attention, but Disguiser is an alternate explanation for the identity in the Lookout report. Check whether Mafia deception is possible and whether a second result supports the accusation before calling it impossible.
