Town of Salem mechanics

Use the rule reference to separate a legal interaction from a false claim. These pages cover the original Town of Salem, including its Coven expansion.

Follow the evidence from night to trial

Start with the action that produced the information. A visit list answers a different question from a Sheriff result, and a guilty vote uses a different threshold from a nomination. Mixing those systems is a common source of accusations against real Town roles.

Check the language before arguing about the result

A will often mixes an observation with a conclusion. “I saw Player 4 visit” reports a claimed observation; “Player 4 is confirmed” adds an interpretation. Compare the exact statement with the ability that could produce it before using it to close a role slot.
