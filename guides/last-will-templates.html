Town of Salem Last Will Templates

Copy-ready will formats for Sheriff, Investigator, Lookout, Doctor, Bodyguard, Spy, Transporter, Tavern Keeper, Retributionist and Jailor.

These Town of Salem last will templates are starting formats. Replace every bracketed field with what actually happened. The layouts below are intentionally short enough to scan during a trial.

Copy-ready role formats

Sheriff

Sheriff
N1 - checked [number] - [exact result]
N2 - checked [number] - [exact result]
Reads (not results): [separate notes]

Keep innocent and suspicious as results; never silently convert innocent into confirmed Town.

Investigator

Investigator
N1 - [number] - [full role group]
N2 - [number] - [full role group]
Possible transport/frame/douse: [night and reason]

Record the full group rather than replacing it with the role the player later claimed.

Lookout

Lookout
N1 - watched [number] - visitors [numbers]
N2 - watched [number] - visitors [numbers]
Extra unnamed visitors: [night, if reported]

Record unnamed additional visitors when the three-person limit is reached. A disguise remains a possible identity exception.

Doctor or Bodyguard

[Doctor / Bodyguard]
N1 - protected [number] - feedback [message/none]
N2 - protected [number] - feedback [message/none]
Self-protection used: [night / not used]

Name the actual role. “Nobody died” is not equivalent to receiving a successful-protection message.

Spy

Spy
N1 - bug [number]: [feedback]
MV: [target numbers] | CV: [target numbers, Coven only]
N2 - bug [number]: [feedback]
MV: [target numbers] | CV: [target numbers, Coven only]

Bug feedback and faction visits answer different questions. Keep the two streams on separate lines.

Transporter and roleblockers

Transporter
N1 - swapped [number] and [number]
N2 - swapped [number] and [number]

Tavern Keeper
N1 - blocked [number] - [feedback]
N2 - blocked [number] - [feedback]

A Transporter needs both recipients. Tavern Keeper is the renamed Escort; old shorthand may still appear in chat.

Retributionist

Retributionist
N2 - used [dead number/role] on [living number]
Result: [exact feedback]
Used corpses: [numbers]

The corpse and the recipient are different targets. Do not write this as a permanent resurrection.

Jailor

Jailor
N1 - jailed [number] - claim [role]
N2 - jailed [number] - [executed / spared]
Executions remaining: [count]
Reason for execution: [brief evidence]

Keep the prisoner’s claim separate from confirmation. Share sensitive targets only when disclosure helps Town.

Preserve corrections visibly

If you mistype a target, mark the correction rather than silently rebuilding earlier nights to fit a new claim. The point of a will is a trustworthy chronology, not a perfect-looking story. Keep speculation below the mechanical log.

Using the game’s saved templates

The original game added last-will templates to Customization in patch 3.3.11. Save a blank format for the roles you play often, then fill it during the match. Copying these examples requires no clipboard permission; select the relevant block and use your normal copy command.
