Stickman games

Stickman games put simple line characters at the center of fast, skill-based challenges. In this category, you control a stick figure through action-packed runs, dodging hazards and collecting boosts. The focus is on quick reactions and steady progress, with each attempt pushing you to improve your timing and route. Whether you are sprinting through history or facing new obstacles, Stickman games reward precision and persistence.
