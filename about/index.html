About Town of Salem

Welcome to Town of Salem, a multiplayer game of deduction and deception where every match is a new puzzle. Our core experience revolves around 48 distinct roles, each with its own abilities and alignment. Before each game, players gather in a lobby where the host selects which roles will be in play. From that list, each player is randomly assigned a role, ensuring that no two games are ever the same.

When the game begins, you receive a role card that explains your character's powers and goals, helping you plan your strategy and dive into the action. With over 300 achievements to unlock, there's always a new challenge to pursue. Earn Merit Points as you unlock achievements, adding a layer of progression to your gameplay. Our community is active and engaged, with comments and ratings enabled so you can share your experiences and feedback.

We are committed to providing a fair and enjoyable environment for all players. Join us in the town where trust is scarce and every decision matters.
