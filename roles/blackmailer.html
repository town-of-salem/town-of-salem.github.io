🔪 Mafia Support

Blackmailer

Silence a target and distort the information available to Town.

MafiaMafia SupportTown of Salem role guide
Quick answer: Choose one player at night to blackmail, preventing that player from speaking during the next day.
Alignment / classMafia Support
Primary jobRemove key voices from the discussion while helping Mafia control both information and the elimination agenda

Silence, Mafia coordination and promotion

  • The silenced player cannot contribute normal day chat.
  • You share Mafia night communication.
  • If Mafia loses its kill-capable roles, support roles can be promoted into the killing chain under classic Mafia rules.

Silence information before it is shared

Early game

Blackmail players whose speech is producing Town structure: a confirmed investigator, a strong claim organizer or someone coordinating whispers. Silencing a random quiet player wastes the role’s unique advantage.

Mid game

Use blackmail to separate information from influence. If Town already knows a player’s result, silencing them may matter less than suppressing the person who is connecting several results into a coherent case.

Endgame

A blackmailed Town player keeps their vote even though ordinary speech is restricted. Time blackmail around a day where Mafia needs confusion more than another fake claim.

Keep the cover separate from the blackmail

As Mafia, your public claim must explain your night activity without accidentally matching a known blackmail pattern. Avoid fake claims that require visible actions on the exact player you silenced.

Compare silence with the Mafia’s interests

From Town’s perspective, repeated silence around the same strategic moments can reveal what Mafia fears. A Blackmailer fake claim may also know private communication details too conveniently.

Important interactions

  • Spy faction-visit logs can expose that Mafia visited the blackmailed player.
  • Mayor’s public vote power can remain dangerous even when normal communication is disrupted, so blackmailing the Mayor is not always sufficient control.
  • Consigliere information can help the Blackmailer pick the player whose silence will remove the most valuable claim.

Decision example: blackmailer target choice

A confirmed Investigator has already posted their Night 1 result, but a second Town player is coordinating all claims and exposing inconsistencies. Blackmailing the coordinator may create more day control than silencing the Investigator whose existing result is already public.

Whispers are a second information source

Blackmailer can read whispers between other players. Use that information for Mafia decisions without assuming Town has seen the same conversation. Quoting a private target or exact role publicly can expose access you should not have under your cover claim.

For Town, whispering to a trusted recipient does not make the message secret from an active Blackmailer. Share the minimum detail needed for the immediate plan and preserve important conclusions in the public record when they become relevant to a vote.

Common mistakes

  • Blackmailing players who were already contributing nothing.
  • Using the same target pattern until Spy or Lookout evidence points back to the Mafia plan.
  • Building a fake claim that contradicts the public effects of your own blackmail.
