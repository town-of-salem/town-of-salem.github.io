Jester

Get yourself eliminated by Town without making the plan too obvious.

NeutralNeutral EvilTown of Salem role guide
Quick answer: Win by getting the Town to eliminate you, then punish one guilty or abstaining voter on the following night.
Alignment / classNeutral Evil
Primary jobAppear suspicious enough to earn a trial and guilty vote while staying plausible enough that the lobby believes the elimination helps them

A hanging wins; an ordinary death does not

  • The victory condition requires a day elimination rather than merely dying.
  • After being eliminated, the Jester may haunt one of the players who voted guilty or abstained under classic rules.
  • Being too obviously self-destructive can make Town refuse to give you the win.

Become a plausible suspect before parity

Early game

Create small inconsistencies rather than cartoonish chaos. A strange target, imperfect claim or awkward vote can attract pressure while preserving the possibility that you are simply an evil role making a mistake.

Mid game

Adapt to how cautious the lobby is about Jesters. In experienced games, obvious trolling is a reason not to vote guilty, so controlled contradictions and believable claim-space conflicts work better.

Endgame

A Jester becomes harder to eliminate when every vote threatens parity. Seek a window before factions become unwilling to spend a day on a player whose flip does not directly advance their own win condition.

Make a cover worth questioning

Your claim should be flawed in ways that look like evil trying to survive, not like a player announcing “please eliminate me.” The best false story has enough structure to invite testing and one or two cracks that Town can discover.

Watch the incentive without granting immunity

Town should ask whether the suspicious player is trying to survive the accusation. A claimant who keeps making their story worse after receiving an escape route may be signaling a Jester agenda.

Important interactions

  • Executioner can become Jester if the assigned target dies at night, changing the player’s incentive mid-match.
  • Mayor’s extra votes can push a Jester to trial quickly, but a confirmed Mayor should still consider whether the elimination serves Town.
  • An Investigator’s Framer / Vampire / Jester group can invite pressure. A normal Sheriff result on Jester is innocent; do not confuse the two kinds of investigation.

Decision example: a visiting claim that does not fit

You claim a visiting support role with a target that conflicts with a Lookout log. When challenged, defend the claim just enough to look like caught evil rather than instantly conceding. Town is more likely to vote guilty when it believes it found a real contradiction instead of a volunteer Jester.

Common mistakes

  • Making the Jester objective obvious through random spam or impossible self-accusation.
  • Surviving too cleanly until the lobby is too small to risk the elimination.
  • Forgetting which voters can be haunted after the win.
