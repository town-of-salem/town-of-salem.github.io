GAME GUIDE

About Town Of Salem

Town of Salem is a multiplayer game of deception and deduction where every match is a fresh puzzle. You'll take on one of 48 unique roles, each with its own abilities and alignment, and work to outwit your fellow players. The goal is simple: figure out who is friend and who is foe before it's too late.

The Role Card System

Each game begins in a lobby where the host selects the roles that will be in play. Once the game starts, every player is randomly assigned a role from that list, and you'll receive a role card detailing your character's abilities and alignment. This card is your key to understanding your place in the town and planning your next move.

With over 300 unique achievements to unlock, there's always a new challenge to pursue. Earn Merit Points by completing impressive feats, like burning five people at once as the Arsonist. These achievements can be viewed on your homepage, and the rarest ones are perfect for showing off to your friends.

The heart of Town of Salem lies in the decisions you make. Do you trust the person who claims to be the Doctor? Should you reveal your role to save yourself, or stay silent to avoid suspicion? Every choice can turn the tide, and the social dynamics are as important as the abilities themselves.

FIRST SESSION

How to play Town Of Salem

Start with the basics

Start by joining a lobby and waiting for the host to select the roles for the game. Once the game begins, you'll be assigned a random role and receive a role card that explains your abilities and alignment. Use this information to decide your strategy: work with the Town to find the evil roles, or if you're evil, blend in and deceive. During the day, discuss with other players and vote to lynch suspects. At night, use your abilities if you have any. Pay attention to the actions of others and try to deduce who is lying. The game ends when one faction achieves its goal.

QUICK ANSWERS

Town Of Salem FAQ

What is the objective of Town of Salem?

The objective depends on your role's alignment. Town members must identify and eliminate all evil roles, while evil roles must outnumber or eliminate the Town. Neutral roles have their own win conditions.

How do I know what my role does?

When the game starts, you'll receive a role card that explains your character's abilities and alignment. This card is your primary guide for how to play your role.

Can I play Town of Salem with friends?

Yes, you can join the same lobby as friends. The host can select the roles, and you'll all be randomly assigned roles from that list.

What are Merit Points and how do I earn them?

Merit Points are earned by unlocking Achievements. Achievements are unlocked by performing specific feats, such as burning five people at once as the Arsonist. You can track your progress on your homepage.
