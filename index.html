🔎 What is Town of Salem?

Social deduction with roles, night abilities, a graveyard and public trials

A match has 7–15 players with hidden roles. Town works to remove its enemies; Mafia and Coven coordinate as factions; Neutral roles follow their own victory conditions. The original game and its Coven expansion use different rules from Town of Salem 2.

Night creates private information: investigations, protection, control, attacks, visits and role-specific effects. Day turns those private results into public claims. The game becomes a contest over which story is compatible with the most evidence.

❓ Quick answers

Town of Salem FAQ

What should a new Town of Salem player learn first?

Learn the day/night cycle, your win condition, how to keep a last will, and the difference between a fact, a claim and a read. Then add role-specific strategy.

What does VFR mean in Town of Salem?

VFR means Vote For Role: players use vote pressure to force a claim or will so the town gains public information. It should not be confused with blindly executing every player who reaches trial.

Where can I check Town of Salem Investigator results?

Use the Investigator results guide in the Strategy menu. It separates Classic and Coven result groups and explains how to interpret them against claims and the graveyard.

Are Sheriff results absolute proof?

No. Detection immunity and deception effects mean a suspicious or innocent result should be combined with other mechanical and social evidence.
